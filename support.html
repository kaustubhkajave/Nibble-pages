Support

Email [[SUPPORT EMAIL]] and we'll get back to you. If it's about a specific problem, the Support ID in the app's Settings screen helps us find what happened.

Before you write in

Open Settings in the app and copy your Support ID. It's an anonymous identifier — it tells us nothing about you, but it lets us look up whether a request failed on our side.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they're cancelled through Apple: open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Nibble, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for. There's also a Manage Subscription button inside Nibble's Settings screen that takes you straight there.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID to request one.

I paid but Nibble Plus isn't active

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. That re-checks your Apple ID against our records. If it still doesn't activate, email us with your Support ID.

What does a recipe credit mean?

One credit is one recipe. Asking for three meals uses three credits; asking for one uses one. Creating a single named recipe uses one. Ingredient detection, failed generations, retries, saving recipes, and cooking never use credits.

Using the app

It couldn't make as many meals as I asked for

That's deliberate. Rather than padding out weak suggestions, Nibble gives you the ones that genuinely work and tells you it fell short. Adding a few more ingredients — especially staples like oil, salt, or butter — usually fixes it.

It says my ingredients aren't enough

If "No grocery trip" is on, Nibble will only use what you've confirmed. Turning it off, or confirming a couple of common staples on the ingredients screen, gives it far more to work with.

The ingredient photo missed something

You can add, rename, or remove anything on the ingredient review screen before generating. Only the confirmed list is used.

Where are my saved recipes stored?

On your device only. They aren't synced to a server or to other devices, which also means deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored locally, including the anonymous install identifier. If you'd like server-side records removed as well, email us.

Reporting a problem with a recipe

Nibble uses AI and can get things wrong. Always check ingredients, labels, allergens, and cooking temperatures yourself. If a recipe was unsafe or badly wrong, please tell us what it suggested so we can improve the guardrails.
