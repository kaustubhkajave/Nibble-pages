Subscriptions

Cancel or manage a subscription

Open Nibble's Settings screen and select Nibble Plus, then Manage Subscription. You can also open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select Nibble, and follow the cancellation options. Access continues until the end of the paid period.

Request a refund

Apple handles App Store billing and refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.

Restore Nibble Plus

Open Nibble Plus from the app's Settings screen and tap Restore Purchases. If Plus remains inactive, email us with your Support ID.

Free recipe credits and Nibble Plus

The free version includes a limited number of recipe credits. Each generated meal or named recipe uses one free credit; ingredient photo scanning does not. Nibble Plus includes unlimited recipe generation and photo scans while the subscription is active, subject to service availability and safeguards against misuse.

Using Nibble

The ingredient photo missed something

Add, rename, or remove ingredients on the review screen before generating meals. Only the list you confirm is used.

Nibble created fewer meals than requested

Nibble returns the practical options it can support instead of padding the list. Confirming more ingredients or allowing a grocery trip can make more recipes possible.

A recipe or photo request failed

Check your connection and try again. You can enter ingredients manually if photo analysis remains unavailable. For repeated failures, email the Support ID shown in Settings.

Find or remove saved recipes

Saved recipes are in Nibble's Library and remain on that device. Delete individual recipes from the Library. Deleting the app removes all locally stored Nibble data and cannot be undone.

Safety and privacy

Report an unsafe or incorrect recipe

Email us with the recipe title and the unsafe or incorrect instruction. Nibble uses AI and can make mistakes, so always verify ingredients, allergen labels, doneness, and safe cooking temperatures yourself.

Request deletion of server-side records

Email getnibble@outlook.com and include your Support ID when available. See the privacy policy for what Nibble stores locally and what is processed by the service.
