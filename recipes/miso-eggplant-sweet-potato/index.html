Miso Eggplant & Sweet Potato

Miso-glazed eggplant steaks on a buttery sweet potato purée, with a soy-mirin glaze and pickled scallion curls.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 large globe eggplant, about 1 lb (450 g)
  • 1 medium sweet potato, about 10 oz (300 g), peeled and cubed
  • 2 tbsp white miso
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin, divided
  • 3 scallions, green parts only
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided, plus salt for the cooking water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Boil the sweet potato in salted water for 12–15 minutes until soft. Drain and blend with the butter, a pinch of salt and a splash of water until smooth. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Slice the scallion greens into thin strips and soak in iced water for 10 minutes to curl. Drain and toss with the rice vinegar.

  3. 03

    Cut the eggplant lengthwise into 4 thick steaks, score the cut sides in a crosshatch and salt lightly. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat and cook for 5–6 minutes per side until golden and completely tender.

  4. 04

    Mix the miso, 1 tablespoon mirin and the sugar. Brush it over the top of the eggplant, cover the pan and cook for 1–2 minutes until it bubbles.

  5. 05

    In a small pan, simmer the soy sauce with the remaining mirin for about 1 minute until syrupy.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Sweet potato purée

    Spoon about 3 tablespoons of sweet potato purée slightly left of center and sweep it into a thick curved swoosh.

  2. Step 2

    First eggplant steak

    Lay one eggplant steak flat on the right side of the purée.

  3. Step 3

    Second eggplant steak

    Overlap a second steak on the first, angled upward toward the top of the plate.

  4. Step 4

    Soy-mirin glaze

    Spoon 1 teaspoon of soy-mirin glaze over the center of the top steak.

  5. Step 5

    Pickled scallion curls

    Place a light cluster of scallion curls on the glazed steak.
