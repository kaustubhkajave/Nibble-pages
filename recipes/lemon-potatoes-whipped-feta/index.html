Lemon Potatoes & Whipped Feta

Crisp golden potato rounds over a bright spinach-dill purée, with whipped feta, fresh dill and lemon zest.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) Yukon Gold potatoes, sliced into 1/2-inch (1.25 cm) rounds
  • 5 oz (150 g) baby spinach
  • 1 small bunch fresh dill, a few fronds reserved
  • 1/2 yellow onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 oz (115 g) feta cheese
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3 tbsp water, plus water for boiling
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus salt for boiling

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the potato rounds in salted water for 6–8 minutes until just tender. Drain and steam-dry for 5 minutes.

  2. 02

    Meanwhile, cook the onion and garlic in 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat for 4 minutes. Blanch the spinach and most of the dill in boiling water for 30 seconds, drain and squeeze dry. Blend with the onion, half the lemon juice, 2 tbsp water and a pinch of salt until silky.

  3. 03

    Blend the feta with 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp water until smooth and whipped.

  4. 04

    Heat the remaining oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the potatoes for 4–5 minutes per side until deep golden and crisp. Season with salt.

  5. 05

    Spread the spinach purée in a circle on each plate. Overlap the potatoes in an arc across it, add three dollops of whipped feta along the inner curve, then finish with dill fronds and grated lemon zest.
