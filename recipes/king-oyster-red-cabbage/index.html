King Oyster & Red Cabbage

Golden seared king oyster mushrooms on a magenta red cabbage purée, with garlic-parsley breadcrumbs and lemon butter.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 4 king oyster mushrooms, about 10 oz (300 g)
  • 1/4 small red cabbage, about 10 oz (300 g), shredded
  • 3 garlic cloves, 1 minced and 2 smashed
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 lemon
  • Small bunch of flat-leaf parsley, 8 small leaves reserved
  • 1/3 cup (25 g) panko or dry breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided, plus salt for the cooking water
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the cabbage in salted water for about 12 minutes until very soft. Drain, keeping a little of the water, and blend with 1 tablespoon butter, the juice of half the lemon and 2–3 tablespoons of the water until smooth. The lemon keeps it bright magenta. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Melt 1 tablespoon butter in a skillet over medium heat. Toast the breadcrumbs with the minced garlic for 2–3 minutes, stirring, until golden. Stir in 1 tablespoon chopped parsley and a pinch of salt, then transfer to a bowl.

  3. 03

    Halve the mushrooms lengthwise and score the cut sides in a crosshatch. Season with the remaining salt and the pepper.

  4. 04

    Heat the oil in the skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the mushrooms cut-side down for 4–5 minutes until deep golden, then turn and cook for 2 minutes.

  5. 05

    Add the remaining butter, the smashed garlic and a squeeze of lemon, and spoon the butter over the mushrooms for 1 minute. Keep the pan juices for plating.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Red cabbage purée

    Spoon about 4 tablespoons of cabbage purée onto the upper left of each plate and swipe the back of the spoon through it to make a thick arc.

  2. Step 2

    Seared king oyster mushrooms

    Lay 3 mushroom halves, crosshatch-side up, overlapping along the arc.

  3. Step 3

    Garlic-parsley breadcrumbs

    Spoon 2 tablespoons of breadcrumbs in a tidy cluster along the lower edge of the mushrooms.

  4. Step 4

    Parsley leaves

    Tuck 4 parsley leaves into the gaps between the mushrooms.

  5. Step 5

    Lemon-butter pan juices

    Spoon 1 teaspoon of the lemon-butter pan juices over the tops of the mushrooms only.
