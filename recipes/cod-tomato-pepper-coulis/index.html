Cod & Tomato-Pepper Coulis

Golden seared cod over a charred tomato and red pepper sauce, with a briny olive-caper relish and cilantro oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 thick cod fillets, about 6 oz (170 g) each
  • 3 roma tomatoes, halved
  • 1 red bell pepper, quartered
  • 1/4 cup (35 g) green olives, chopped
  • 1 tbsp capers, chopped
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped red onion
  • 1 small bunch cilantro
  • 1 lime
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat a dry skillet over high heat and char the tomatoes and pepper for 8–10 minutes, turning, until blackened in spots and soft. Blend with 1 tbsp olive oil, half the lime juice and a pinch of salt until smooth. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Mix the olives, capers, red onion and 1 tbsp olive oil into a chunky relish.

  3. 03

    Blend most of the cilantro with 1 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt until bright green, then strain through a fine sieve.

  4. 04

    Pat the cod dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat the remaining oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat and cook for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and the fish flakes easily or reaches 145°F (63°C).

  5. 05

    Pool the coulis in each shallow bowl, set the cod in the center at an angle, spoon the relish along its lower edge, scatter with cilantro and add a few drops of cilantro oil around the sauce.
