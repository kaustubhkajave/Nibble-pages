Pork Loin & Dijon Cauliflower

Seared pork loin slices on a silky Dijon cauliflower purée, with sweet glazed shallots, pan juices and parsley.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless pork loin chops, about 1 lb (450 g) total and 3/4 inch (2 cm) thick
  • 1/2 head cauliflower, about 14 oz (400 g), cut into florets
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 4 shallots, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided, plus salt for the cooking water
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) water, plus a splash

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the cauliflower in salted water for 10–12 minutes until very soft. Drain well and blend with the cream, Dijon, 1 tablespoon butter and 1/4 teaspoon salt until silky. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Cook the shallots in the remaining butter over medium-low heat for about 10 minutes until soft and golden. Add the sugar and a splash of water and cook for 2 minutes until glossy.

  3. 03

    Pat the pork dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and sear for 3–4 minutes per side, until the center reaches 145°F (63°C). Rest for 3 minutes, then slice.

  4. 04

    Add 1/4 cup water to the pork pan and scrape up the browned bits for about 1 minute to make the pan juices.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Dijon cauliflower purée

    Spoon half the cauliflower purée into the center of each plate and smooth it into a clean 5-inch (13 cm) round.

  2. Step 2

    Seared pork loin

    Overlap the slices of 2 pork chops diagonally across the center of the purée.

  3. Step 3

    Glazed shallots

    Mound 2 tablespoons of glazed shallots over the top of the pork.

  4. Step 4

    Pan juices

    Spoon 1 tablespoon of pan juices in a thin ring around the purée.

  5. Step 5

    Parsley

    Sprinkle 1/2 teaspoon parsley over the shallots and pork.
