Cod & Creamed Leeks

Golden pan-seared cod on silky creamed leeks, with lemon brown butter, crispy capers and parsley.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 cod fillets, 5–6 oz (150–170 g) each
  • 2 leeks, white and light green parts, thinly sliced and rinsed
  • 1/3 cup (80 ml) heavy cream
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp capers, drained and patted dry
  • 1 lemon
  • Small bunch of flat-leaf parsley, 6 small leaves reserved
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Melt 1 tablespoon butter in a pan over medium-low heat. Add the leeks and a pinch of salt and cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring often, until soft but not browned. Add the cream and simmer for 2–3 minutes until thick. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the capers for 1–2 minutes until they burst and crisp, then lift them onto a paper towel, keeping the oil in the pan.

  3. 03

    Pat the cod dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Sear in the caper oil for 3–4 minutes until golden, then turn and cook for 2–3 minutes until the flesh is opaque and flakes easily, or reaches 145°F (63°C).

  4. 04

    Wipe out the pan. Melt the remaining butter over medium heat for 1–2 minutes until it smells nutty and turns golden. Off the heat, add 1 tablespoon lemon juice and 1 tablespoon chopped parsley.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Creamed leeks

    Spoon half the creamed leeks into a neat 4-inch (10 cm) round, slightly left of center on each plate.

  2. Step 2

    Pan-seared cod

    Set one cod fillet across the top of the leeks, golden side up.

  3. Step 3

    Lemon brown butter

    Spoon 1 tablespoon of brown butter in a crescent around the lower right of the leeks.

  4. Step 4

    Crispy capers

    Scatter 1 tablespoon of crispy capers over the cod.

  5. Step 5

    Parsley leaves

    Place 3 small parsley leaves on the cod, keeping the rim clean.
