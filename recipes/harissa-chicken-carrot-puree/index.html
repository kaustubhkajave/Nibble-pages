Harissa Chicken & Carrot Purée

Crisp harissa-orange glazed chicken thighs over a silky carrot and orange purée, with charred onion and cilantro-mint oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
45 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, about 12 oz (340 g)
  • 1 lb (450 g) carrots, 1 reserved and shaved into ribbons, the rest chopped
  • 1 red onion, half chopped and half cut into thin wedges
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 1/2 tbsp harissa paste
  • 1 small bunch cilantro
  • 1 small bunch fresh mint, a few small leaves reserved
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 tsp salt, divided, plus salt for boiling

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the chopped carrots, chopped onion and garlic in salted water for 15 minutes until very soft. Drain and blend with the juice of one orange and 2 tbsp olive oil until silky.

  2. 02

    Pat the chicken dry and season with the remaining salt. Cook skin-side down in 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat for 10–12 minutes until deeply golden. Turn and cook for 8–10 minutes more until it reaches 165°F (74°C).

  3. 03

    Mix the harissa with the juice of half the second orange. Brush over the chicken skin and cook for 1 minute more until shiny. Set aside.

  4. 04

    Char the onion wedges and carrot ribbons in the pan for 2–3 minutes. Blend most of the cilantro and mint with the remaining oil and strain.

  5. 05

    Swoosh the purée across each plate, set the chicken skin-side up on top, tuck the charred onion and carrot at its lower left, drizzle herb oil along the curve and finish with small mint leaves.
