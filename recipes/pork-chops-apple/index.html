Pork Chops & Apple

Seared pork chops over buttery mash, with golden apples, onions and a glossy thyme pan sauce.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 bone-in pork chops, about 225 g each and 2 cm thick
  • 500 g Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 3 cm pieces
  • 1 large apple, cored and thinly sliced
  • 1/2 yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 40 g unsalted butter, divided
  • 60 ml heavy cream
  • 6 fresh thyme sprigs, divided
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 80 ml water, plus water for boiling
  • 3/4 tsp salt, plus salt for potato water
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Cover the potatoes with cold salted water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 15–18 minutes until easily pierced. Drain, steam-dry for 1 minute and mash with 25 g butter and the cream. Season to taste and keep covered.

  2. 02

    Pat the pork dry and season with 1/2 teaspoon salt and the pepper. Heat the oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Sear for 4–5 minutes per side, then lower the heat and continue as needed until the center reaches 63°C / 145°F, away from the bone.

  3. 03

    Transfer the pork to a warm plate and rest for at least 3 minutes. Lower the skillet heat to medium and add the remaining butter, onion, apple and two thyme sprigs. Cook for 8–10 minutes, turning gently, until golden and tender.

  4. 04

    Add 80 ml water to the skillet and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer for 2–3 minutes until glossy. Remove the cooked thyme stems. Taste and adjust the salt. Keep the apple mixture and pan sauce warm for plating.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Creamy mash

    Divide the mash between two warm plates. Spread each portion into a neat circle slightly left of center.

  2. Step 2

    Seared pork chop

    Rest one pork chop on the right edge of each potato base, with the bone pointing toward two o’clock.

  3. Step 3

    Apple and onion

    Spoon the apple and onion over the center of each chop, letting a few slices fall onto the mash.

  4. Step 4

    Thyme pan sauce

    Spoon the pan sauce in a small crescent around the lower-right edge of each potato base.

  5. Step 5

    Fresh thyme

    Finish each plate with two small thyme sprigs. Set the woody stems aside when eating.
