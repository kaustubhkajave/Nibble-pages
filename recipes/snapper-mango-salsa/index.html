Red Snapper & Mango Salsa

Crisp-skinned red snapper on a roasted red pepper sauce, with mango-jalapeño salsa, tortilla shards and cilantro-lime oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 skin-on red snapper fillets, about 5 oz (150 g) each
  • 2 red bell peppers (or 1 cup / 150 g jarred roasted peppers)
  • 1 ripe mango, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely chopped
  • Small bunch of cilantro, 8 leaves reserved
  • 2 limes
  • 1 flour tortilla
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 5 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Char the bell peppers under a hot broiler, turning, until blistered, or use jarred peppers. Peel, seed and blend with the garlic, 2 tablespoons oil, 1 teaspoon lime juice and 1/4 teaspoon salt until smooth.

  2. 02

    Mix the mango, jalapeño, 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro, the juice of 1 lime and a pinch of salt.

  3. 03

    Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Cut the tortilla into 4 jagged shards, brush with a little oil, sprinkle with salt and bake for 6–8 minutes until golden.

  4. 04

    Chop 1 tablespoon cilantro very finely and stir into 2 tablespoons oil with the zest of the remaining lime.

  5. 05

    Score the fish skin lightly, pat dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook skin-side down for 3–4 minutes until crisp, then turn for 1–2 minutes until the flesh is opaque and flakes easily, or reaches 145°F (63°C).

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Red pepper sauce

    Spoon a glossy swoosh of red pepper sauce from eight o’clock through the center toward one o’clock, leaving the lower right clear.

  2. Step 2

    Tortilla shards

    Lean 2 tortilla shards together at ten o’clock, touching the upper edge of the sauce.

  3. Step 3

    Crisp-skinned snapper

    Set one snapper fillet skin-side up across the center of the sauce.

  4. Step 4

    Mango-jalapeño salsa

    Spoon 3 tablespoons of mango salsa at two o’clock against the fish.

  5. Step 5

    Cilantro-lime oil

    Drizzle 1 teaspoon of cilantro-lime oil over the fish and add 4 cilantro leaves along its top edge.
