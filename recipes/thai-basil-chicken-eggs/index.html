Thai Basil Chicken & Eggs

Garlic-chili chicken and snappy green beans glazed with fish sauce and basil, over jasmine rice with crispy eggs.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (200 g) jasmine rice
  • Water for rice, as directed on its packet
  • 12 oz (340 g) boneless skinless chicken thighs, chopped into small pieces
  • 4 oz (115 g) green beans, cut into 1-inch (2.5 cm) pieces
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1–2 red chilies, thinly sliced
  • 1 1/2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1 large handful Thai basil leaves
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Rinse the rice and cook with the water quantity and timing on its packet. Keep covered.

  2. 02

    Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok or skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the eggs for 2–3 minutes, spooning hot oil over the whites, until the edges are crisp and the whites are fully set. Set aside.

  3. 03

    Add the remaining oil, garlic and chilies and stir for 30 seconds. Add the chicken and stir-fry for 5–6 minutes, then add the green beans and cook for 2–3 minutes more, until the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C).

  4. 04

    Add the fish sauce, sugar and 3 tbsp water and toss for 1 minute until glossy. Take off the heat and stir in the basil until just wilted.

  5. 05

    Spoon the rice onto plates, add the chicken and top each with a crispy egg.
