Sesame Fish & Ginger Ponzu

Crisp sesame-crusted white fish over a glossy ginger-lemon ponzu, with a shaved cucumber and radish salad.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
25 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 white fish fillets, such as cod or haddock, about 6 oz (170 g) each
  • 1/4 cup (35 g) sesame seeds
  • 1 small cucumber, shaved into ribbons
  • 5 radishes, 2 thinly sliced into rounds, the rest shaved
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the soy sauce, ginger, honey, water and the juice of half the lemon in a small pan for 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened and glossy.

  2. 02

    Toss the cucumber and shaved radish with a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt.

  3. 03

    Pat the fish dry, season with salt and press the sesame seeds onto the top side. Heat the oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat and cook sesame-side down for 3–4 minutes until golden. Turn gently and cook for 2–3 minutes more until the fish flakes easily or reaches 145°F (63°C).

  4. 04

    Spoon a circle of ponzu on each plate, place a mound of shaved salad at the left, lay the fish diagonally over it, overlap the radish rounds along the top and drizzle a thin line of ponzu over the crust.
