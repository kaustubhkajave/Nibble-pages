Crispy Gnocchi & Broccolini

Crisp pan-fried gnocchi and seared broccolini on a lemony cannellini bean cream, finished with Parmesan crisps and lemon peel.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 500 g shelf-stable potato gnocchi
  • 1 can cannellini beans, about 400 g, drained and rinsed
  • 1 bunch broccolini, about 200 g, ends trimmed
  • 1 lemon
  • 40 g Parmesan, finely grated
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • About 120 ml water, divided

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F and line a tray with baking paper. Spread the Parmesan into four thin 8 cm rounds. Bake for 5–7 minutes until lacy and golden. Cool on the tray for 5 minutes until crisp, then break into shards. Keep them dry until plating.

  2. 02

    Peel six thin ribbons of lemon peel and set aside, then juice the lemon. Warm the beans with 60 ml water, the garlic and 1 tablespoon olive oil in a small pan for 4–5 minutes. Mash or blend with 1 tablespoon lemon juice and 1/4 teaspoon salt until thick and smooth. Keep warm.

  3. 03

    Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the broccolini, a pinch of salt and 2 tablespoons water, cover and steam for 2 minutes, lifting the lid away from you. Uncover and cook for 2–3 minutes until the stems are tender and the edges browned. Set aside.

  4. 04

    Heat the remaining oil in the skillet over medium-high heat. Add the gnocchi in a single layer with 3 tablespoons water and cover for 2 minutes. Uncover and cook undisturbed for 3–4 minutes until golden underneath, then toss and cook for 2 minutes more until crisp and hot throughout. Season with the remaining salt and the pepper.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Cannellini lemon cream

    Spoon half the bean cream into an oval about 18 × 8 cm slightly below the center of each plate, angled from lower left to upper right.

  2. Step 2

    Seared broccolini

    Lay half the broccolini diagonally across the lower-left half of the cream, florets pointing to the upper right.

  3. Step 3

    Crispy gnocchi

    Arrange about nine gnocchi in a tight, overlapping arc along the upper edge of the cream, browned sides up.

  4. Step 4

    Parmesan crisps

    Stand two Parmesan shards at the upper-right end of the gnocchi arc, leaning lightly against the last gnocchi.

  5. Step 5

    Lemon peel

    Curl three lemon peel ribbons over the center gnocchi, then wipe the rim and serve.
