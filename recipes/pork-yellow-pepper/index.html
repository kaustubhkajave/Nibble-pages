Pork & Yellow Pepper Sauce

Seared pork tenderloin medallions on a sunny yellow pepper sauce, dotted with basil oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 pork tenderloin, about 450 g, trimmed and cut into 6 medallions about 3 cm thick
  • 2 large yellow bell peppers, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 30 g unsalted butter, divided
  • 60 ml heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 25 g basil, plus 8 small leaves to garnish
  • 60 ml olive oil for the basil oil
  • 1 tbsp olive oil for searing
  • 60 ml water
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Dip the 25 g basil in boiling water for 10 seconds, then cool it in cold water and squeeze dry. Blend with 60 ml olive oil and a pinch of salt until smooth, then strain through a fine sieve.

  2. 02

    Melt 15 g butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the yellow peppers, garlic and 1/4 teaspoon salt, and cook for 8–10 minutes until very soft. Add the water and simmer for 2 minutes. Blend with the cream and lemon juice until very smooth, venting the blender lid. The sauce should coat a spoon; simmer briefly to thicken or add water to loosen. Keep warm over low heat.

  3. 03

    Pat the pork dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the medallions for 3–4 minutes per side, adding the remaining butter for the last minute and spooning it over, until the centers reach 63°C / 145°F.

  4. 04

    Rest the pork on a warm plate for 3 minutes before plating.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Yellow pepper sauce

    Spoon half the pepper sauce into the center of each plate and let it settle into a round pool about 12 cm (5 in) across.

  2. Step 2

    Seared pork

    Place two pork medallions, slightly overlapping, in the middle of the sauce.

  3. Step 3

    Seared pork

    Add a third medallion alongside to form a tight cluster.

  4. Step 4

    Basil oil

    Dot six drops of basil oil in a ring around the edge of the sauce.

  5. Step 5

    Basil leaves

    Top the pork with four small basil leaves.
