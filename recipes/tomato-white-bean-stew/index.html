Tomato & Bean Stew

A garlicky white bean and tomato stew with wilted spinach and crisp Parmesan toast.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Total
20 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (425 g each) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (400 g) diced tomatoes
  • 90 g baby spinach
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 40 g grated Parmesan
  • 4 thick slices sourdough bread
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 240 ml water
  • 1/4 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a deep skillet over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and stir for 30–60 seconds until fragrant but not browned.

  2. 02

    Add the tomatoes, beans, water, salt and pepper. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 8–10 minutes. Mash a spoonful of beans against the side of the pan to thicken the broth.

  3. 03

    Meanwhile, brush the bread with the remaining oil. Toast in a second skillet over medium heat for 2–3 minutes per side. Top with half the Parmesan while hot.

  4. 04

    Stir the spinach into the stew and simmer for 2 minutes. Adjust seasoning, divide into bowls and add the remaining Parmesan. Serve with the toast.
