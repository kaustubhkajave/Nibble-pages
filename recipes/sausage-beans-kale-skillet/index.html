Sausage, Beans & Kale Skillet

Seared Italian sausage with creamy cannellini beans, blistered cherry tomatoes and wilted kale, brightened with lemon.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
30 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) Italian sausage links
  • 2 (15-oz) cans (425 g each) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 bunch lacinato kale, stems removed, leaves sliced
  • 1 1/2 cups (225 g) cherry tomatoes
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the sausages for 10–12 minutes, turning, until browned all over and they reach 160°F (71°C). Transfer to a board and slice.

  2. 02

    Add the onion to the pan and cook for 4 minutes. Add the garlic and tomatoes and cook for 3–4 minutes until the tomatoes start to blister.

  3. 03

    Stir in the beans, kale, water, salt and pepper. Cover and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring once, until the kale is tender.

  4. 04

    Return the sausage, squeeze in the juice of half the lemon and toss. Serve with the remaining lemon in wedges.
