Corn Fritters & Avocado

Golden sweet corn fritters on a silky avocado-lime sauce, topped with quick-pickled red onions and fresh cilantro.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 can sweet corn, about 300 g, drained and patted dry
  • 45 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 lime, zested and juiced
  • 1/2 red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 15 g fresh cilantro, a few small leaves reserved
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 2–3 tbsp water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Toss the red onion with half the lime juice and 1/4 teaspoon salt. Let stand for 15 minutes, tossing once, until bright pink.

  2. 02

    Blend the avocado, remaining lime juice, lime zest, most of the cilantro, 1 tablespoon olive oil, 2–3 tablespoons water and 1/4 teaspoon salt until smooth and spoonable.

  3. 03

    Mix the corn, flour, egg, remaining salt and pepper into a thick batter that holds its shape on a spoon.

  4. 04

    Heat the remaining oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Lower in four mounds of batter, about 3 tablespoons each, and flatten them to 1.5 cm. Cook for 3–4 minutes per side until deep golden and the centers are set. Lower the batter in gently to avoid splashes.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Avocado-lime sauce

    Spoon a thick, glossy arc of avocado sauce from the upper left of each plate toward five o’clock.

  2. Step 2

    Corn fritters

    Place one fritter on the lower part of the sauce.

  3. Step 3

    Corn fritters

    Overlap a second fritter slightly above the first.

  4. Step 4

    Pickled red onions

    Arrange five pickled onion ribbons across the top of both fritters.

  5. Step 5

    Cilantro

    Finish with a small cluster of cilantro leaves over the onions on the top fritter.
