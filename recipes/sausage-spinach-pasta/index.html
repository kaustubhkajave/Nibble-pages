Sausage & Spinach Pasta

Penne in a creamy tomato sauce with browned Italian sausage, spinach and Parmesan.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Total
25 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 450 g sweet Italian pork sausage, casings removed
  • 340 g penne
  • 1 can (400 g) diced tomatoes
  • 120 ml heavy cream
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 60 g baby spinach
  • 40 g grated Parmesan
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt for pasta water
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 120 ml reserved pasta cooking water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Boil the penne in salted water according to its packet. Reserve 120 ml cooking water before draining.

  2. 02

    Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Crumble in the sausage and cook for 7–9 minutes, breaking it up, until browned and at least 71°C / 160°F.

  3. 03

    Reduce the heat to medium. Stir in the garlic for 30 seconds. Add the tomatoes with their juices and the cream. Simmer gently for 5 minutes.

  4. 04

    Fold in the cooked pasta and spinach. Toss for 1–2 minutes until wilted. Remove from the heat, add Parmesan and pepper, then loosen with reserved pasta water a splash at a time.
