Mushroom Risotto

Creamy garlic and thyme risotto topped with golden seared cremini mushrooms, fresh thyme and Parmesan.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup (150 g) Arborio rice
  • 3 cups (720 ml) hot vegetable broth
  • 8 oz (225 g) cremini mushrooms
  • 1/2 cup (40 g) finely grated Parmesan, 2 tbsp reserved
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 small shallot, finely chopped
  • Small bunch of thyme
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Halve 6 small mushrooms and slice the rest. Heat the oil and 1 tablespoon butter in a skillet over high heat. Sear the mushrooms for 5–6 minutes until golden, then add half the garlic, 1 teaspoon thyme leaves and a pinch each of salt and pepper. Set aside.

  2. 02

    Melt 1 tablespoon butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Soften the shallot and the remaining garlic for 3 minutes, then add the rice and stir for 1 minute.

  3. 03

    Add the hot broth one ladle at a time, stirring often and letting each addition absorb before the next, for 18–20 minutes until the rice is creamy and just tender.

  4. 04

    Off the heat, stir in the Parmesan, except the 2 tablespoons for the top, the remaining butter, half the sliced mushrooms and the remaining salt. Rest for 1 minute.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Mushroom risotto

    Spoon half the risotto into a neat 5-inch (13 cm) circle in the center of each plate, tapping the plate to settle it.

  2. Step 2

    Sliced mushrooms

    Lay 4 mushroom slices overlapping in an arc along the upper right of the risotto.

  3. Step 3

    Seared mushroom halves

    Place 3 mushroom halves cut-side up in a tight cluster in the center.

  4. Step 4

    Thyme leaves

    Scatter 1/2 teaspoon thyme leaves over the mushrooms.

  5. Step 5

    Parmesan

    Dust 1 tablespoon of Parmesan over the risotto, keeping the rim clear.
