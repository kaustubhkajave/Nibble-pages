Beef Bibimbap Bowl

Gochujang beef, garlicky spinach, carrots and mushrooms over rice, topped with a crisp fried egg.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
35 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups (300 g) short-grain or medium-grain white rice
  • 1 lb (450 g) ground beef
  • 2 tbsp gochujang, plus more to serve
  • 2 carrots, cut into thin matchsticks
  • 8 oz (225 g) mushrooms, sliced
  • 5 oz (150 g) baby spinach
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided
  • Water for rice, as directed on its packet
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Rinse the rice and cook with the water quantity and timing on its packet. Keep covered.

  2. 02

    Heat 1 tsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the carrots for 2 minutes, then set aside. Cook the mushrooms for 4–5 minutes until golden, then set aside. Wilt the spinach with a third of the garlic and a pinch of salt for 1 minute.

  3. 03

    Add the beef and remaining garlic to the skillet and cook for 6–8 minutes, breaking it up, until browned and it reaches 160°F (71°C). Stir in the gochujang and a splash of water until glossy.

  4. 04

    Wipe the skillet, heat the remaining oil over medium-high heat and fry the eggs for 2–3 minutes until the edges are crisp and the whites are fully set. Cook longer if you prefer firm yolks.

  5. 05

    Divide the rice among bowls. Arrange the beef, carrots, mushrooms and spinach on top, add an egg to each and serve with extra gochujang.
