Shrimp & Cannellini Purée

Seared garlicky shrimp over silky white bean purée, with red pepper sauce, charred cherry tomatoes and basil oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 10 oz (285 g) large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 (15-oz) can (425 g) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 red bell peppers, chopped
  • 1 cup (150 g) cherry tomatoes, half chopped and half halved
  • 1 handful baby spinach
  • 1 small bunch fresh basil
  • 3 garlic cloves, divided
  • 5 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) water, plus water for blanching
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the peppers, chopped tomatoes and 1 sliced garlic clove in 1 tbsp olive oil and 2 tbsp water for 12 minutes until soft. Blend until smooth and season with salt.

  2. 02

    Warm the beans with 1 garlic clove and 2 tbsp water for 3 minutes, then blend with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt and pepper until velvety.

  3. 03

    Blanch the spinach and basil for 10 seconds, squeeze dry and blend with 2 tbsp olive oil, then strain.

  4. 04

    Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over high heat. Char the halved tomatoes, cut side down, for 2 minutes and set aside. Season the shrimp with salt and the last garlic clove, minced, and sear for 1 1/2–2 minutes per side until pink and opaque.

  5. 05

    Spread the bean purée in a round on each plate, spoon the pepper sauce over its upper half, arrange the shrimp in a crescent, add the charred tomatoes at the lower left and drizzle with basil oil.
