Chicken, Corn & Red Pepper

Golden chicken medallions over a smooth red pepper purée, with buttery sweet corn and chives.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts, about 400 g total
  • 2 red bell peppers: 1 1/2 chopped, 1/2 finely diced
  • 300 g sweet corn kernels, thawed if frozen or drained if canned
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced, divided
  • 30 g unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 60 ml water
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped chives
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the chopped pepper, half the garlic and a pinch of salt, and cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring, until very soft. Add the water and simmer for 2 minutes. Blend with 15 g butter until smooth, venting the blender lid. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Cut each chicken breast crosswise into 3 thick pieces and press each gently to about 2 cm thick so they cook evenly. Season with 1/2 teaspoon salt and the pepper.

  3. 03

    Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken for 4–5 minutes per side until golden and the center reaches 74°C / 165°F. Rest on a warm plate for 3 minutes.

  4. 04

    Lower the heat to medium and melt the remaining butter in the skillet. Add the corn, diced pepper, remaining garlic and remaining salt. Sauté for 3–4 minutes until hot and glossy.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Red pepper purée

    Spoon half the red pepper purée onto the center of each plate and spread it into an offset oval with the back of the spoon.

  2. Step 2

    Seared chicken

    Overlap three chicken medallions across the middle of the purée, running from ten to four o’clock.

  3. Step 3

    Sweet corn

    Spoon some of the corn along the upper-left edge of the chicken.

  4. Step 4

    Sweet corn

    Spoon the rest of the corn along the lower-right edge of the chicken.

  5. Step 5

    Chives

    Scatter the chives over the chicken and corn.
