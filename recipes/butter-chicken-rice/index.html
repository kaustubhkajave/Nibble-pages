Butter Chicken & Rice

Yogurt-marinated chicken simmered in a silky tomato, butter and cream gravy, served with fluffy basmati rice.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
45 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1/2 cup (120 g) plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp garlic-ginger paste
  • 2 tsp garam masala, divided
  • 3 tbsp (40 g) unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 (14.5-oz) can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 cups (300 g) basmati rice
  • Water for rice, as directed on its packet
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) water
  • 1 1/4 tsp salt, divided

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Mix the chicken with the yogurt, garlic-ginger paste, 1 tsp garam masala and 1/2 tsp salt. Rest for 10 minutes while you start the rice.

  2. 02

    Rinse the rice and cook with the water quantity and timing on its packet. Keep covered.

  3. 03

    Melt 2 tbsp butter in a deep skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken, shaking off excess marinade, and cook for 5–6 minutes, turning, until lightly browned.

  4. 04

    Stir in the crushed tomatoes, 1/4 cup (60 ml) water, the remaining garam masala and salt. Simmer for 10–12 minutes, stirring, until the sauce thickens and the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C).

  5. 05

    Lower the heat, stir in the cream and the remaining butter until glossy. Taste and adjust the salt. Serve over the rice.
