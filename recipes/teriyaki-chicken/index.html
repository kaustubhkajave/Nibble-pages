Teriyaki Chicken

Glossy teriyaki chicken with a neat rice dome, charred green onions and toasted sesame.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 large boneless skin-on chicken thighs, about 350 g total
  • 180 g short-grain white rice
  • Water for rice, as directed on its packet
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 4 green onions
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 2 tbsp water for the glaze
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Rinse the rice and cook with the water quantity and timing on its packet. Once cooked, rest covered off the heat for 5 minutes. Thinly slice the green top of one onion for garnish; cut the rest into 7 cm lengths.

  2. 02

    Pat the chicken dry and season with pepper. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the thighs skin-side down and cook for 7–9 minutes, pressing gently at first, until the skin is golden. Turn and cook for 5–7 minutes more, until the thickest part reaches 74°C / 165°F.

  3. 03

    Transfer the chicken to a clean plate. Pour off excess fat, leaving about 1 teaspoon in the skillet. Cook the onion lengths over medium-high heat for 2–3 minutes, turning, until lightly charred. Set aside.

  4. 04

    Lower the heat. Add soy sauce, mirin, sugar and 2 tablespoons water. Simmer for 2–3 minutes until lightly syrupy. Return the chicken and turn briefly to coat, then transfer to a board. Save the remaining glaze.

  5. 05

    Slice each thigh across the grain into five or six pieces. Fluff the rice. Have two lightly oiled small bowls ready to shape the rice, and keep the glaze warm.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Rice dome

    Divide the rice between two small bowls, press gently and invert each onto the lower-left area of a plate. Lift away the bowls.

  2. Step 2

    Glazed chicken

    Fan one sliced chicken thigh against the upper-right slope of each rice dome. Keep the glazed skin facing up.

  3. Step 3

    Charred green onion

    Lay three charred green onion lengths across each chicken fan, angled toward the top-right of the plate.

  4. Step 4

    Teriyaki glaze

    Spoon the remaining glaze in a shallow crescent along the right base of each chicken fan.

  5. Step 5

    Sesame finish

    Scatter the sesame seeds and reserved sliced green onion tops over the chicken. Serve any remaining onions alongside.
