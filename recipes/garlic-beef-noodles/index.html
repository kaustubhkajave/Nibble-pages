Garlic Beef Noodles

Slippery noodles tossed with garlicky beef, tender bok choy and plenty of green onion.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Total
20 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 225 g dried ramen noodles, seasoning packets discarded
  • 450 g ground beef
  • 3 heads baby bok choy, stems and leaves chopped separately
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 120 ml reserved noodle cooking water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook the noodles according to their packet. Reserve 120 ml cooking water, then drain.

  2. 02

    Heat the oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the beef for 6–8 minutes, breaking it apart, until it reaches 71°C / 160°F. Spoon off excess fat.

  3. 03

    Reduce to medium. Add the garlic and bok choy stems; stir-fry for 2 minutes. Add the leaves, soy sauce and 60 ml noodle water. Cook for 2 minutes until the greens wilt.

  4. 04

    Add the noodles and toss for 1–2 minutes to coat. Loosen with the remaining noodle water if needed. Finish with pepper and sliced green onions.
