Za'atar Cauliflower Steak

A thick za'atar-crusted cauliflower steak on silky sweet potato purée, with pink pickled onion, parsley oil and lemon zest.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 large head cauliflower
  • 1 large sweet potato (about 400 g), peeled and cubed
  • 2 tbsp za'atar
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced into thin rings
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 cup (30 g) parsley leaves
  • 2 garlic cloves, divided
  • 5 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Toss the onion rings with the juice of half the lemon and a pinch of salt. Set aside for 15 minutes until pink.

  2. 02

    Boil the sweet potato in salted water for 12–15 minutes until very soft. Drain, then blend with 1 garlic clove, 1 tablespoon oil and 1/4 teaspoon salt until smooth. Keep warm.

  3. 03

    Trim the cauliflower stem and cut two 1-inch (2.5 cm) steaks from the center. Brush with 1 tablespoon oil and press the za'atar, 1/4 teaspoon salt and the pepper onto both sides.

  4. 04

    Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Sear the steaks for 4–5 minutes per side until deep golden, then cover and cook for 3–4 minutes more until a knife slides easily into the core.

  5. 05

    Blend the parsley with the remaining 2 tablespoons oil, the remaining garlic clove, a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt until bright green.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Sweet potato purée

    Spoon about 4 tablespoons of sweet potato purée left of center and swoosh it with the back of the spoon into a thick 5-inch (13 cm) curve.

  2. Step 2

    Za'atar cauliflower steak

    Set one cauliflower steak over the middle of the swoosh, angled slightly toward one o’clock.

  3. Step 3

    Pickled red onion

    Tuck 4 curls of pickled onion along the top edges of the cauliflower at eleven and one o’clock.

  4. Step 4

    Parsley oil

    Spoon 1 tablespoon of parsley oil over the browned florets, letting a drop pool beside the purée.

  5. Step 5

    Lemon zest

    Finish with a pinch of lemon zest over the cauliflower.
