Shrimp & Lemony Orzo

Lemony orzo with an arc of seared shrimp, soft zucchini ribbons, a burst cherry tomato sauce and crunchy toasted breadcrumbs.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 340 g large shrimp, about 12, peeled and deveined
  • 150 g orzo
  • 1 medium zucchini
  • 200 g cherry tomatoes
  • 1 lemon
  • 30 g panko or dry breadcrumbs
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided, plus salt for the pasta water
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Toast the breadcrumbs with 1 tablespoon olive oil in a skillet over medium heat for 3–4 minutes, stirring, until golden. Tip them out and season with a pinch of salt and a little lemon zest.

  2. 02

    Cook the orzo in salted boiling water for 8–10 minutes until tender. Shave the zucchini into long ribbons with a peeler. Dip the ribbons into the boiling pot for the last 30 seconds, then lift them out and drain. Drain the orzo and toss it with 1 tablespoon lemon juice, the remaining zest, 1 teaspoon olive oil and 1/4 teaspoon salt. Keep warm.

  3. 03

    Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in the skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tomatoes, garlic and 1/4 teaspoon salt. Cook for 5–6 minutes until the tomatoes burst, pressing some to make a chunky sauce. Tip into a bowl.

  4. 04

    Pat the shrimp dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat the remaining oil in the skillet over high heat. Sear in a single layer for 1–2 minutes per side until pink, opaque and firm, 63°C / 145°F. Keep raw shrimp away from cooked food.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Lemon orzo

    Spoon half the orzo into a compact oval about 15 × 8 cm, slightly below the center of each plate.

  2. Step 2

    Seared shrimp

    Arrange five or six shrimp in a tight, overlapping arc along the upper edge of the orzo, curved sides facing out.

  3. Step 3

    Zucchini ribbons

    Drape three zucchini ribbons in loose parallel folds across the lower third of the orzo.

  4. Step 4

    Burst tomato sauce

    Spoon the burst tomato sauce into a shallow pool along the right edge of the orzo.

  5. Step 5

    Toasted breadcrumbs

    Sprinkle a tablespoon of toasted breadcrumbs in a narrow line over the shrimp, then wipe the rim. Follow the thermometer guidance in the cooking steps, regardless of the color shown in the image.
