Turkey Cutlet & Squash Purée

Golden seared turkey cutlets over silky butternut squash purée, with glazed green apple, red onion and crisp kale.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
45 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 turkey cutlets, about 6 oz (170 g) each
  • 1 lb (450 g) butternut squash, peeled and diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 green apple, cut into wedges
  • 1/2 red onion, cut into wedges
  • 4 lacinato kale leaves
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/3 cup (80 ml) water, plus water for boiling
  • 1 tsp salt, divided, plus salt for boiling
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the squash and garlic in salted water for 12–15 minutes until very soft. Drain and blend with 1 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt until silky.

  2. 02

    Season the turkey with salt and pepper. Sear in 1 tbsp oil over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and it reaches 165°F (74°C). Rest for 3 minutes, then slice.

  3. 03

    In the same pan, cook the apple and onion with 1 tbsp oil for 3 minutes, add the water and cook for 3–4 minutes more until soft and glazed. Spoon out, leaving the juices as a pan glaze.

  4. 04

    Wipe a small pan, heat the remaining oil and fry the kale leaves for 30–60 seconds until crisp. Drain and salt lightly.

  5. 05

    Swoosh the squash purée across each plate, fan the turkey slices over the lower half, nestle the apple and onion above them, tuck in the crisp kale and drizzle the pan glaze over the turkey.
