Thai Pork Belly & Curry

Slow-roasted crackling pork belly on a pool of golden yellow curry sauce, with pickled chili shallots and Thai basil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
105 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) skin-on pork belly
  • 1 can (13.5 oz / 400 ml) coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp yellow curry paste
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 2 shallots, thinly sliced
  • 2 red chilies, sliced
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • Small bunch of Thai basil
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • Pinch of sugar

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Pat the pork skin very dry, score it with a sharp knife and rub with 1 teaspoon salt. Roast skin-side up at 325°F (160°C) for 75 minutes.

  2. 02

    Turn the oven up to 450°F (230°C) and roast for 15–20 minutes more, until the skin puffs and crackles and the meat reaches at least 145°F (63°C). Rest for 10 minutes, then cut into thick slabs.

  3. 03

    While the pork roasts, toss the shallots and half the chili with the rice vinegar, a pinch of sugar and a pinch of salt.

  4. 04

    Fry the curry paste in the oil in a small saucepan for 1 minute. Add the coconut milk, fish sauce and brown sugar and simmer for 8–10 minutes until thick enough to coat a spoon.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Yellow curry sauce

    Spoon about 4 tablespoons of curry sauce into the center and spread it into a wide 5-inch (13 cm) pool.

  2. Step 2

    Crispy pork belly

    Shingle 3 thick slabs of pork belly diagonally across the sauce.

  3. Step 3

    Pickled chili shallots

    Scatter 6 slices of pickled shallot over the top right of the pork.

  4. Step 4

    Pickled chili shallots

    Place 4 rings of fresh red chili over the shallot and pork.

  5. Step 5

    Thai basil

    Crown the center of the pork with 5 Thai basil leaves.
