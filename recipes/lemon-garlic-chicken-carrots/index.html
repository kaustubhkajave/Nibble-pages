Lemon Garlic Chicken

Golden chicken thighs cooked with carrots and garlic, finished with wilted spinach, lemon and a spoonful of lemon yogurt.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, about 1 1/2 lb (680 g)
  • 3 medium carrots, sliced into coins
  • 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 2 large handfuls baby spinach, about 3 oz (85 g)
  • 1 lemon
  • 1/4 cup (60 g) Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) water
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Pat the chicken dry and season with the salt and pepper. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat and cook skin-side down for 8–10 minutes, without moving, until golden and crisp.

  2. 02

    Turn the chicken and add the carrots, garlic and water. Cover and cook for 10–12 minutes, until the carrots are tender and the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C) near the bone.

  3. 03

    Zest the lemon into the yogurt and stir. Lift the chicken out, add the spinach to the pan and stir until just wilted. Squeeze in half the lemon.

  4. 04

    Plate the chicken with the carrots and spinach and add a spoonful of lemon yogurt on the side.
