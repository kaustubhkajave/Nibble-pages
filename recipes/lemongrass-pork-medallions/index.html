Lemongrass Pork Medallions

Caramelized lemongrass pork over a bright red chili-lime sauce, with crisp pickled carrot and daikon ribbons.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340 g) boneless pork loin, cut into 6 medallions
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, tender core finely minced
  • 2 red chilies, 1 seeded and chopped, 1 thinly sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 limes
  • 1 carrot, shaved into ribbons
  • 3-inch (8 cm) piece daikon radish, shaved into ribbons
  • 2 tsp sugar, divided
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) water
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Toss the carrot and daikon ribbons with the juice of one lime, 1 tsp sugar and a pinch of salt. Set aside to pickle lightly.

  2. 02

    Simmer the chopped chili, bell pepper and water for 6–8 minutes until soft. Blend with the juice of the second lime and a pinch of salt until smooth and glossy.

  3. 03

    Rub the pork with the lemongrass, remaining sugar and salt. Sear in the oil over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes per side until caramelized and it reaches 145°F (63°C). Rest for 3 minutes.

  4. 04

    Add a splash of water to the pan and scrape up the browned bits into a quick glaze.

  5. 05

    Smear the chili sauce diagonally across each plate, overlap three pork medallions on top, pile the pickled ribbons beside them, drizzle the pan glaze over the pork and finish with sliced chili.
