Steak & Poblano Purée

Seared flank steak on a sweep of charred poblano purée, with smoky corn, quick-pickled red onion and cilantro.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340 g) flank steak
  • 2 poblano peppers
  • 1 cup (150 g) corn kernels, thawed if frozen
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 limes
  • 1/2 cup (15 g) cilantro, plus 10 small leaves to finish
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 2–4 tbsp water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Toss the onion with the juice of 1 lime, the sugar and a pinch of salt. Set aside for at least 15 minutes, tossing once.

  2. 02

    Char the poblanos directly over a gas flame or under a hot broiler, turning, for 8–10 minutes until blackened all over. Cover in a bowl for 5 minutes, then peel off the skin and remove the stems and seeds.

  3. 03

    Blend the poblanos with the garlic, cilantro, 2 tablespoons oil, the juice of half a lime, 1/4 teaspoon salt and 2 tablespoons water until completely smooth, adding more water a spoonful at a time if needed. Keep warm.

  4. 04

    Heat a dry skillet over high heat. Char the corn for 4–5 minutes, stirring only occasionally, until spotted brown. Season with a pinch of salt and a squeeze of lime and transfer to a bowl.

  5. 05

    Pat the steak dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat the remaining oil in the skillet over high heat and sear for 3–4 minutes per side, until the thickest part reaches 145°F (63°C). Rest for at least 3 minutes, then slice against the grain.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Poblano purée

    Spoon about 3 tablespoons of poblano purée left of center and drag the spoon through it to make a 5-inch (13 cm) diagonal teardrop.

  2. Step 2

    Seared flank steak

    Shingle 3 slices of steak over the thick end of the purée, overlapping them so each seared edge shows.

  3. Step 3

    Charred corn

    Spoon 2 tablespoons of charred corn beside the steak toward one o’clock, letting a few kernels scatter.

  4. Step 4

    Pickled red onion

    Drape 4 strands of pickled onion over the steak and beside it at eleven o’clock.

  5. Step 5

    Cilantro leaves

    Finish with 5 small cilantro leaves over the steak and onion.
