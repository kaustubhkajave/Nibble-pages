Steak, Romesco & Asparagus

Seared steak over a silky roasted pepper and almond romesco, with charred asparagus, toasted almonds and flaky salt.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 strip or sirloin steak, about 12 oz (340 g) and 1 inch (2.5 cm) thick
  • 2 red bell peppers, quartered
  • 1 tomato, halved
  • 1/3 cup (40 g) almonds, plus 1 tbsp sliced almonds
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
  • 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided, plus flaky salt to finish
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Char the peppers and tomato in a dry skillet over high heat for 8–10 minutes until blistered and soft. Toast the almonds and sliced almonds in the pan for 2 minutes. Blend the peppers, tomato, whole almonds, garlic, vinegar, 3 tbsp olive oil and 1/2 tsp salt until silky.

  2. 02

    Pat the steak dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Sear in the remaining oil over high heat for 3–4 minutes per side, until it reaches 130–135°F (54–57°C) for medium-rare or 145°F (63°C) for medium.

  3. 03

    Rest the steak for 5 minutes. Meanwhile, char the asparagus in the same pan for 3 minutes until bright green and tender-crisp.

  4. 04

    Slice the steak into thick pieces across the grain.

  5. 05

    Spread the romesco into a bold oval on each plate. Fan the steak over its upper half, lay the asparagus diagonally beside it, scatter the toasted sliced almonds over the asparagus and finish the steak with flaky salt.
