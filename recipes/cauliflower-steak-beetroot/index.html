Cauliflower Steak & Beetroot

Golden cauliflower steaks on a vivid beetroot purée, with parsley oil and toasted pumpkin seeds.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
45 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 large cauliflower, about 1 kg
  • 250 g cooked beetroot (not pickled), roughly chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 40 g unsalted butter, divided
  • 20 g flat-leaf parsley, plus 6 small leaves to garnish
  • 60 ml olive oil for the parsley oil
  • 1 tbsp olive oil for searing
  • 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F. Remove the cauliflower leaves and trim the stem flat, keeping the core intact. Stand it upright and cut two 2.5 cm (1 in) thick steaks from the center. Save the trimmings for another dish.

  2. 02

    Toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry ovenproof skillet over medium heat for 2–3 minutes, shaking the pan, until lightly golden and starting to pop. Tip onto a plate.

  3. 03

    Dip the 20 g parsley in boiling water for 10 seconds, then cool it in cold water and squeeze dry. Blend with 60 ml olive oil and a pinch of salt until smooth, then strain through a fine sieve.

  4. 04

    Season the cauliflower steaks on both sides with 1/2 teaspoon salt and the pepper. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil and 20 g butter in the skillet over medium-high heat. Sear for 3–4 minutes per side until deep golden, then transfer the skillet to the oven for 12–15 minutes until a knife slides easily into the core.

  5. 05

    While the cauliflower roasts, melt the remaining 20 g butter in a small saucepan over medium-low heat and cook the garlic for 1 minute. Add the beetroot and 2 tablespoons water and warm through for 3–4 minutes. Blend with the lemon juice and the remaining salt until very smooth, adding water a tablespoon at a time until it holds a soft swoosh. Keep warm.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Beetroot purée

    Spoon half the beetroot purée onto each plate at six o’clock and drag the back of the spoon upward to form a curved teardrop toward twelve o’clock.

  2. Step 2

    Cauliflower steak

    Set a cauliflower steak over the wide lower end of the purée, golden side up.

  3. Step 3

    Parsley oil

    Dot the parsley oil in six small drops along the outer curve of the purée.

  4. Step 4

    Pumpkin seeds

    Scatter the toasted pumpkin seeds over the top of each cauliflower steak.

  5. Step 5

    Parsley leaves

    Finish with three small parsley leaves along the center of each steak.
