Chicken & Turmeric Bean Purée

Seared chicken medallions on a golden turmeric butter bean purée, with blistered tomatoes, parsley oil and lemon zest.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz / 400 g) butter beans, drained
  • 1 large boneless, skinless chicken breast, about 10 oz (280 g)
  • 8 cherry tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • Large handful of flat-leaf parsley (about 1/2 cup / 10 g), a few tiny leaves reserved
  • 1 lemon
  • 6 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Warm the beans with the garlic, turmeric, 2 tablespoons oil and the water for 5 minutes. Blend until silky with 1 tablespoon lemon juice and 1/4 teaspoon salt. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Blend the parsley with 3 tablespoons oil and a pinch of salt, then strain.

  3. 03

    Cut the chicken crosswise into 6 thick medallions and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and sear for 3–4 minutes per side, until they reach 165°F (74°C) in the center.

  4. 04

    Blister the tomatoes in the same pan for about 3 minutes, then halve them.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Turmeric bean purée

    Spoon about 4 tablespoons of bean purée across the upper left and center of each plate and swipe it into a thick curve.

  2. Step 2

    Seared chicken medallions

    Shingle 3 chicken medallions along the center of the purée, crust-side up.

  3. Step 3

    Blistered tomatoes

    Arrange 4 tomato halves along the right side of the chicken.

  4. Step 4

    Parsley oil

    Drizzle 2 teaspoons of parsley oil over the chicken and the edges of the tomatoes.

  5. Step 5

    Lemon zest and parsley

    Finish with a pinch of lemon zest and a few tiny parsley leaves on the chicken.
