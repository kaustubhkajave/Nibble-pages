Halloumi & Pea Purée

Golden seared halloumi over a bright minted pea purée, with lemony radishes, warm chili oil and fresh mint.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
20 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 300 g frozen peas
  • 250 g halloumi, cut into 6 slices about 1 cm thick
  • 10 g fresh mint leaves, 6 small leaves reserved
  • 4 radishes, thinly sliced
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 60 ml reserved pea cooking water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Boil the peas for 2–3 minutes. Reserve 60 ml of the cooking water, then drain. Blend the hot peas with the mint, 1 tablespoon lemon juice, 2 tablespoons olive oil and the salt, adding the reserved water a spoonful at a time until thick and silky. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Toss the radish slices with 1 teaspoon lemon juice and a pinch of salt. Let stand for 5 minutes.

  3. 03

    Warm 1 tablespoon olive oil with the red pepper flakes over low heat for about 1 minute until fragrant, then take off the heat.

  4. 04

    Pat the halloumi dry. Heat the remaining oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Sear for 1–2 minutes per side until deep golden. Moisture can make the oil spit. Plate right away; halloumi firms as it cools.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Minted pea purée

    Spoon half the pea purée onto the center left of each plate and curve it into a thick oval with the back of the spoon.

  2. Step 2

    Seared halloumi

    Overlap three halloumi slices on a diagonal across the center of the purée.

  3. Step 3

    Lemony radishes

    Tuck five radish slices along the right edge of the halloumi.

  4. Step 4

    Chili oil

    Spoon a teaspoon of chili oil over the top of the halloumi.

  5. Step 5

    Mint and lemon zest

    Finish with three small mint leaves and a pinch of lemon zest over the halloumi.
