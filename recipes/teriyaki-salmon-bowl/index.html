Teriyaki Salmon Bowl

Crisp-skinned salmon in a garlic-ginger honey soy glaze, with wilted spinach over fluffy white rice.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 skin-on salmon fillets, about 6 oz (170 g) each
  • 1 cup (200 g) white rice
  • Water for rice, as directed on its packet
  • 5 oz (150 g) baby spinach
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 1/2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Rinse the rice and cook with the water quantity and timing on its packet. Keep covered. Stir the soy sauce, honey, ginger, half the garlic and 2 tbsp water together.

  2. 02

    Pat the salmon dry and season with the salt. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and cook skin-side down for 4–5 minutes until crisp. Flip and cook for 2 minutes.

  3. 03

    Pour in the sauce and simmer for 2–3 minutes, spooning it over the fish, until glossy and the salmon flakes easily or reaches 145°F (63°C). Lift the salmon out.

  4. 04

    Add the spinach and remaining garlic to the pan and toss for 1 minute until just wilted.

  5. 05

    Spoon the rice into bowls, top with the spinach and salmon, and drizzle with the remaining glaze.
