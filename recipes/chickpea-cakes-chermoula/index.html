Chickpea Cakes & Chermoula

Crisp spiced chickpea cakes on a ras el hanout carrot purée, with fresh herb chermoula and pomegranate.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz / 400 g) chickpeas, drained and patted dry
  • 3 medium carrots, about 10 oz (300 g), sliced
  • 1 cup (20 g) cilantro leaves
  • 1/2 cup (10 g) mint leaves
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp ras el hanout, divided
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp pomegranate seeds
  • 8 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 2–4 tbsp water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the carrots with 1 garlic clove in water for 12–15 minutes until soft. Drain and blend with 2 tablespoons oil, 1/2 teaspoon ras el hanout, 1 teaspoon lemon juice, 1/4 teaspoon salt and 2–4 tablespoons water until smooth. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Pulse the chickpeas in a food processor with 1 garlic clove, the remaining ras el hanout, a few cilantro sprigs, the flour and 1/2 teaspoon salt until the mixture holds together. Shape into 4 cakes about 3/4 inch (2 cm) thick.

  3. 03

    Heat 3 tablespoons oil in a skillet over medium heat. Fry the cakes for 3–4 minutes per side until crisp and golden.

  4. 04

    Finely chop the remaining cilantro and the mint. Mix with the last garlic clove (grated), the juice of half the lemon, the remaining oil and a pinch of salt.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Spiced carrot purée

    Spoon about 3 tablespoons of carrot purée on the left of each plate and sweep it into a thick swoosh across the upper half.

  2. Step 2

    Chickpea cakes

    Set one chickpea cake on the widest part of the purée.

  3. Step 3

    Chickpea cakes

    Overlap a second cake slightly below the first.

  4. Step 4

    Chermoula

    Spoon 2 teaspoons of chermoula over the cakes, letting a little trail onto the purée.

  5. Step 5

    Pomegranate seeds

    Scatter 8 pomegranate seeds over the cakes and chermoula.
