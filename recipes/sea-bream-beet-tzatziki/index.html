Sea Bream & Beet Tzatziki

Crisp-skinned sea bream on a magenta beet tzatziki, with dill cucumber relish, Kalamata olives and lemon zest.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
25 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 sea bream fillets, skin on (about 5 oz / 140 g each)
  • 1 small cooked beet, finely grated
  • 3/4 cup (180 g) Greek yogurt
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated
  • 1 cucumber, finely diced
  • 4 Kalamata olives, halved
  • 1 lemon
  • Small bunch of dill
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Stir the beet, garlic, a squeeze of lemon and 1/4 teaspoon salt into the yogurt until evenly pink.

  2. 02

    Mix the cucumber with chopped dill, a squeeze of lemon, 1 tablespoon oil and a pinch of salt. Keep 3 small dill sprigs for garnish.

  3. 03

    Score the fish skin 3 times, pat dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper.

  4. 04

    Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Lay the fish in skin-side down and press flat for the first 30 seconds. Cook for 3–4 minutes until the skin is crisp, then turn for about 1 minute until it flakes easily and reaches 145°F (63°C).

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Beet tzatziki

    Spoon a thick 5-inch (13 cm) arc of beet tzatziki across the center and upper left of the plate.

  2. Step 2

    Pan-seared sea bream

    Set the fish skin-side up diagonally over the tzatziki, from ten to four o’clock.

  3. Step 3

    Cucumber relish

    Spoon 2 tablespoons of cucumber relish along the lower edge of the fish.

  4. Step 4

    Kalamata olives

    Place 4 olive halves around the base of the fish at seven and two o’clock.

  5. Step 5

    Dill and lemon zest

    Finish with 3 dill sprigs and a little lemon zest on the fish skin.
