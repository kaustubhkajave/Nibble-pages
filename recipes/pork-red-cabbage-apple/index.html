Pork, Red Cabbage & Apple

Pork tenderloin medallions on a magenta red cabbage purée, with butter-caramelized apple wedges and chives.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 pork tenderloin, about 450 g, trimmed and cut into 6 medallions about 3 cm thick
  • 300 g red cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 tart green apple, cored and cut into 6 wedges
  • 40 g unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 150 ml water, plus 2 tbsp for the pan juices
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped chives
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Put the cabbage, 150 ml water, vinegar and 1/4 teaspoon salt in a saucepan. Cover and simmer for 12–15 minutes until very soft, adding a splash of water if it runs dry. Blend with 15 g butter and the cream until smooth, venting the blender lid and adding cooking liquid as needed. Keep warm. The vinegar keeps the purée magenta rather than blue.

  2. 02

    Pat the pork dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and sear the medallions for 3–4 minutes per side until the centers reach 63°C / 145°F. Rest on a warm plate for at least 3 minutes.

  3. 03

    Lower the heat to medium and add 15 g butter to the skillet. Add the apple wedges cut side down, sprinkle with the sugar and cook for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and just tender. Move them next to the pork.

  4. 04

    Add 2 tablespoons water, the remaining 10 g butter and any resting juices to the skillet. Swirl and scrape up the browned bits for about 30 seconds until glossy.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Red cabbage purée

    Spoon half the cabbage purée onto the left side of each plate and smooth it into an oval.

  2. Step 2

    Seared pork

    Overlap three pork medallions diagonally across the purée, from seven to two o’clock.

  3. Step 3

    Caramelized apples

    Tuck three apple wedges beside the pork at four o’clock, skin side facing out.

  4. Step 4

    Pan juices

    Spoon a teaspoon of the glossy pan juices over the pork.

  5. Step 5

    Chives

    Scatter the chives over the pork and purée.
