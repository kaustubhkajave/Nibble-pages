Chicken, Spinach & Parmesan

Sliced seared chicken breast on a Parmesan spinach purée, with blistered cherry tomatoes and a crisp Parmesan wafer.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, about 1 lb (450 g) total
  • 4 cups (120 g) baby spinach
  • 6 cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup (40 g) finely grated Parmesan, divided
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 2–3 tbsp water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Make 2 small mounds of Parmesan, about 2 tablespoons each, on a lined baking sheet. Bake for 5–7 minutes until golden and leave to cool and crisp.

  2. 02

    Wilt the spinach with 1 sliced garlic clove in 1 tablespoon oil for about 2 minutes. Blend with the remaining Parmesan, 2–3 tablespoons water and a pinch of salt until smooth. Keep warm.

  3. 03

    Pound the chicken to an even thickness and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and cook for 5–6 minutes per side, until it reaches 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part. In the last minute, add the butter and the remaining garlic clove, smashed, and spoon the butter over the chicken.

  4. 04

    Rest the chicken for 5 minutes, then slice it crosswise.

  5. 05

    Blister the tomatoes in the same pan for about 3 minutes.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Parmesan spinach purée

    Spoon most of the spinach purée into a compact oval just left of center on each plate, keeping a teaspoon back. Leave the rim clean.

  2. Step 2

    Seared chicken breast

    Fan the slices of one chicken breast diagonally across the lower edge of the purée.

  3. Step 3

    Blistered tomatoes

    Nestle 3 blistered tomatoes in a tight cluster at the upper right of the purée.

  4. Step 4

    Parmesan crisp

    Lean a Parmesan crisp against the center of the chicken.

  5. Step 5

    Parmesan spinach purée

    Dot 3 small drops of the reserved purée beyond the tomatoes to finish the arc, then wipe the rim.
