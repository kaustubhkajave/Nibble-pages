Mushroom Stroganoff

Golden cremini mushrooms and onion in a tangy sour cream and Dijon sauce, tossed with egg noodles and dill.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
30 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340 g) wide egg noodles
  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 cup (240 g) sour cream, at room temperature
  • 3 tbsp chopped fresh dill
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) noodle cooking water
  • 1 tsp salt, plus salt for the pasta water
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Cook the noodles in well-salted boiling water according to the packet. Reserve 1/2 cup (120 ml) of the cooking water, then drain.

  2. 02

    Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and onion and cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring only now and then, until the liquid has evaporated and the mushrooms are deeply browned.

  3. 03

    Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Lower the heat to low and stir in the mustard, salt, pepper and half the noodle water.

  4. 04

    Take the pan off the heat and stir in the sour cream so it doesn't split. Add the noodles and toss, loosening with more noodle water until glossy. Scatter with dill and serve.
