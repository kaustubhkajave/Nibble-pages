Crispy Polenta & Red Pepper

A golden seared polenta slab on silky roasted red pepper sauce, with goat cheese, toasted pine nuts and bright basil oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
45 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 120 g quick-cook polenta
  • 500 ml water
  • 1 jar roasted red peppers, about 200 g drained
  • 50 g soft goat cheese
  • 20 g fresh basil leaves
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 2 tbsp pine nuts
  • 5 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Oil a small dish about 15 cm square. Bring the water and 1/2 teaspoon salt to a boil. Whisk in the polenta slowly and cook, stirring, for 3–5 minutes until very thick. Spread it 2 cm thick in the dish, cool briefly, then chill uncovered for 20 minutes until firm.

  2. 02

    Blend the red peppers, garlic, 1 tablespoon olive oil, a pinch of salt and the black pepper until silky. Warm gently in a small pan and keep warm.

  3. 03

    Blend the basil with 3 tablespoons olive oil and a pinch of salt until bright green. Toast the pine nuts in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes, shaking the pan, until golden, then tip them out.

  4. 04

    Cut the set polenta into two rectangles about 10 × 6 cm and pat dry. Heat the remaining oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Sear for 4–5 minutes per side without moving until a deep golden crust forms. The oil may spit.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Red pepper sauce

    Spoon a quarter cup of warm red pepper sauce into the center of each plate and spread it into a smooth 12 cm circle.

  2. Step 2

    Crispy polenta

    Set one polenta slab on the upper half of the sauce at a slight diagonal.

  3. Step 3

    Goat cheese

    Place four crumbles of goat cheese across the lower half of the slab and onto the sauce.

  4. Step 4

    Toasted pine nuts

    Scatter about ten toasted pine nuts around the edge of the slab and over the sauce.

  5. Step 5

    Basil oil

    Drizzle a teaspoon of basil oil over the top of the polenta.
