Chicken & Tarragon Mushrooms

Sliced pan-seared chicken breast on a creamy tarragon mushroom sauce, with golden cremini mushrooms and fresh tarragon.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts (about 1 lb / 450 g)
  • 8 oz (225 g) cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) chicken stock
  • 1/3 cup (80 ml) heavy cream
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh tarragon, plus 5 whole leaves
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Pat the chicken dry and season with 1/2 teaspoon salt and the pepper. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and sear for 5–6 minutes per side until the thickest part reaches 165°F (74°C).

  2. 02

    Rest the chicken on a warm plate for 5 minutes, then slice on the diagonal.

  3. 03

    In the same pan, melt the butter and brown the mushrooms for 5 minutes. Set half aside for plating.

  4. 04

    Add the shallot and garlic to the remaining mushrooms for 1 minute, then pour in the stock and simmer for 2 minutes. Stir in the cream and Dijon and simmer until the sauce coats a spoon.

  5. 05

    Stir in the chopped tarragon and the remaining salt.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Tarragon mushroom sauce

    Spoon about 4 tablespoons of sauce into a neat 4-inch (10 cm) pool in the center.

  2. Step 2

    Pan-seared chicken breast

    Fan 5 slices of chicken across the sauce from eight to two o’clock, overlapping evenly.

  3. Step 3

    Golden mushrooms

    Spoon 3 tablespoons of reserved mushrooms along the upper right edge of the chicken.

  4. Step 4

    Tarragon mushroom sauce

    Drizzle 1 tablespoon of sauce across the middle of the chicken, leaving the seared edges visible.

  5. Step 5

    Tarragon leaves

    Finish with 5 tarragon leaves on the chicken and mushrooms and keep the rim clean.
