Trout & Tomato Sugo

Crisp-skinned trout over a glossy garlic and butter tomato sugo, finished with fried capers, fresh basil and basil oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Get the Nibble app
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 skin-on trout fillets, about 6 oz (170 g) each
  • 1 (14.5-oz) can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp (30 g) unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp capers, drained and patted dry
  • 1 small bunch fresh basil, 6 small leaves reserved
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Melt 1 tbsp butter with 1 tbsp olive oil in a small saucepan over medium heat. Cook the garlic for 1 minute, add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt and simmer for 12–15 minutes, stirring, until thick and glossy.

  2. 02

    Blend most of the basil with 2 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt, then strain to a smooth green oil.

  3. 03

    Melt the remaining butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the capers for 1–2 minutes until they open and crisp. Lift out onto paper towel.

  4. 04

    Pat the trout very dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Add the remaining oil to the skillet and cook skin-side down for 3–4 minutes, pressing gently at first, until the skin is crisp. Turn and cook for 30–60 seconds, until the fish flakes easily or reaches 145°F (63°C).

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Tomato sugo

    Spoon 3 tablespoons of sugo onto the left side of each plate and drag it into a thick curved swoosh toward the bottom.

  2. Step 2

    Crisp-skinned trout

    Lay one trout fillet skin-side up at an angle across the center of the sugo.

  3. Step 3

    Fried capers

    Scatter the crispy capers along the top ridge of the trout.

  4. Step 4

    Fresh basil

    Tuck three small basil leaves around the edges of the fillet.

  5. Step 5

    Basil oil

    Drizzle a teaspoon of basil oil over the trout and onto the sugo.
