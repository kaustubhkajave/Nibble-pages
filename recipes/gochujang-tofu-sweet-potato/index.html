Gochujang Tofu & Sweet Potato

Crisp kimchi-gochujang glazed tofu over a silky gochujang sweet potato purée, with charred peppers, bok choy and sesame.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
45 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 14 oz (400 g) firm tofu, pressed and cut into 2 thick rectangles
  • 1 large sweet potato, about 12 oz (340 g), peeled and cubed
  • 2 tbsp gochujang, divided
  • 1/2 cup (75 g) kimchi, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into strips
  • 2 baby bok choy, halved
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) water, plus water for boiling
  • 1/4 tsp salt, plus salt for boiling

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the sweet potato in salted water for 12–15 minutes until very tender. Drain, reserving a little water, and blend with 1 tbsp gochujang and enough cooking water to make a smooth purée.

  2. 02

    Toast the sesame seeds in a dry skillet until fragrant and set aside. Heat 1 tbsp oil over medium-high heat and sear the tofu for 3–4 minutes per side, without moving, until deeply golden.

  3. 03

    Move the tofu to the side. Cook the garlic and kimchi for 2 minutes, add the remaining gochujang and 2 tbsp water and turn the tofu in the glaze until lacquered. Set aside.

  4. 04

    Add the remaining oil and char the pepper strips for 2–3 minutes. Add the bok choy and 2 tbsp water, cover and cook for 2 minutes until bright green and tender.

  5. 05

    Swoosh the purée across each plate. Set the tofu at the top of it, lean the peppers against the tofu, add a bok choy half and a spoonful of kimchi, then scatter sesame seeds over the tofu.
