Tikka Cauliflower Steak

A golden tikka-spiced cauliflower steak over bright mint yogurt, with charred red onion and a ruby pomegranate glaze.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 large head cauliflower
  • 2 tsp tikka masala spice blend
  • 1/2 cup (120 g) Greek yogurt
  • 1 small bunch fresh mint
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 pomegranate
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Stand the cauliflower on its stem and cut two 1-inch (2.5 cm) steaks from the center. Rub with 1 tbsp oil, the tikka spices and half the salt.

  2. 02

    Blanch the mint for 10 seconds, squeeze dry and blend with the yogurt, garlic, half the lemon juice and a pinch of salt until smooth and green.

  3. 03

    Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the steaks for 5–6 minutes per side, adding 2 tbsp water and covering for the last 3 minutes, until deeply golden and tender. Squeeze over the remaining lemon.

  4. 04

    Char the onion in the remaining oil for 4 minutes. Simmer half the pomegranate seeds with 1 tbsp water for 3 minutes, pressing them, then strain into a glossy glaze.

  5. 05

    Swoosh the mint yogurt into an oval on each plate, lay the cauliflower steak across it, add the charred onion at the top right, spoon the pomegranate glaze along the lower edge and scatter the remaining seeds.
