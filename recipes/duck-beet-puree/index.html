Duck Breast & Beet Purée

Crisp-skinned duck breast over a silky beet purée, with orange-honey green beans and caramelized shallots.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
50 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 duck breasts, about 8 oz (225 g) each
  • 2 medium beets, about 10 oz (285 g), peeled and diced
  • 6 oz (170 g) green beans, trimmed
  • 2 shallots, sliced into rings
  • 1 orange
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt, divided, plus salt for boiling
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the beets in salted water for 20–25 minutes until very soft. Drain, reserving a little water, and blend with the olive oil, a pinch of salt and the pepper until silky.

  2. 02

    Score the duck skin in a crosshatch without cutting the meat and season with the remaining salt. Place skin-side down in a cold skillet and cook over medium heat for 10–12 minutes, pouring off the fat now and then, until the skin is crisp.

  3. 03

    Turn the duck and cook for 2–4 minutes until it reaches 135°F (57°C) for pink, or 165°F (74°C) if you follow USDA guidance. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice.

  4. 04

    Pour off all but 1 tbsp fat. Cook the shallots for 3 minutes, add the green beans for 3 minutes, then the juice of the orange and the honey. Toss for 1 minute until glossy. Spoon out the beans and shallots and reduce the juices to a light glaze.

  5. 05

    Spoon the beet purée into a pool on each plate, fan the duck slices across it skin-up, set the green beans alongside, scatter the shallot rings and drizzle the orange-honey glaze over the duck.
