Shrimp, Polenta & Red Pepper

Butter-seared shrimp on crisp golden polenta rounds, over a creamy roasted red pepper coulis with scallion curls.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
55 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 6 jumbo shrimp, about 250 g, peeled and deveined
  • 75 g quick-cooking polenta
  • 300 ml water
  • 150 g jarred roasted red peppers, drained
  • 60 ml heavy cream
  • 60 g unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 2 scallions, green parts only
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Line a small dish, about 15 × 12 cm, with baking paper. Bring the water and 1/2 teaspoon salt to a boil, then whisk in the polenta in a thin stream. Cook, stirring, for 3–5 minutes or as the packet directs, until very thick. Stir in 15 g butter, spread about 1.5 cm thick in the dish and smooth the top. Refrigerate for 30 minutes until firm enough to cut.

  2. 02

    Cut the scallion greens into 8 cm lengths, then slice them lengthwise into very thin strips. Soak in ice water for 10 minutes until they curl, then drain and pat dry.

  3. 03

    Blend the roasted peppers with the cream until very smooth. Warm in a small saucepan with 10 g butter over low heat for 2–3 minutes and season with a pinch of salt. Simmer a few minutes longer if it needs thickening to hold a swoosh.

  4. 04

    Cut two rounds from the polenta with a 7–8 cm cutter or glass. Heat the oil and 10 g butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat and sear the rounds for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and crisp. Keep warm.

  5. 05

    Pat the shrimp dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Add 10 g butter to the skillet and cook the shrimp for 1–2 minutes per side until pink and opaque all the way through. Lower the heat, add the remaining butter and the garlic, and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. Take off the heat.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Red pepper coulis

    Spoon half the coulis onto each plate and swoop it clockwise into a smooth arc from eight to two o’clock.

  2. Step 2

    Polenta round

    Set a polenta round on the lower end of the coulis at about seven o’clock.

  3. Step 3

    Seared shrimp

    Shingle three shrimp from the polenta up along the curve of the coulis.

  4. Step 4

    Garlic butter

    Spoon the warm garlic butter over the shrimp.

  5. Step 5

    Scallion curls

    Pile the scallion curls over the shrimp and polenta.
