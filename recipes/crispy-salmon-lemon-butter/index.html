Crispy Salmon & Lemon Butter

Crisp-skinned salmon over buttery mashed potatoes and green beans, finished with a lemon butter pan sauce.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 skin-on salmon fillets, about 170 g each
  • 500 g Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 3 cm pieces
  • 200 g green beans, trimmed
  • 60 ml heavy cream, warmed
  • 60 g unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 large lemon: 4 thin slices cut from the middle, the rest juiced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided, plus more for the potato water
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Cover the potatoes with cold salted water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil and simmer for 12–15 minutes until very tender. Drain, return to the pan over low heat for 1 minute to steam dry, then mash with 30 g butter, the warm cream and 1/4 teaspoon salt until smooth. Cover and keep warm.

  2. 02

    Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the green beans, a pinch of salt and 2 tablespoons water. Cover and cook for 4 minutes, then uncover and sauté for 2–3 minutes until tender-crisp and the water has evaporated. Move to a warm plate.

  3. 03

    Pat the salmon very dry and season with 1/4 teaspoon salt and the pepper. Wipe out the skillet and heat the remaining oil over medium-high heat. Lay the fillets in skin-side down and press gently for 10 seconds so the skin stays flat. Cook for 4–5 minutes without moving until the skin is crisp, then flip and cook for 1–3 minutes until the center reaches 63°C / 145°F. Transfer skin-side up to a plate.

  4. 04

    Pour off any fat and lower the heat to low. Add the remaining 30 g butter, 2 tablespoons lemon juice and 1 tablespoon water. Swirl the pan for about 1 minute until glossy, without letting it boil hard. Season with a pinch of salt.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Mashed potatoes

    Spoon half the mash into the center of each plate and shape it into a neat round mound about 10 cm (4 in) across.

  2. Step 2

    Green beans

    Lay the green beans side by side across the left side of the mash, letting the ends rest on the plate.

  3. Step 3

    Crispy salmon

    Place a salmon fillet skin-side up diagonally across the beans and the mash.

  4. Step 4

    Lemon butter sauce

    Spoon the lemon butter sauce into a pool around the right side of the mash.

  5. Step 5

    Lemon slices

    Finish each fillet with two overlapping lemon slices and serve right away.
