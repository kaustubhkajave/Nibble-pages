Steak & Sweet Potato

Seared steak over silky sweet potato, with bright parsley chimichurri and golden garlic chips.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 6 beef tenderloin medallions, about 75 g each and 2 cm thick
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, about 500 g, peeled and diced
  • 25 g flat-leaf parsley, very finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, 2 minced and 1 thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 30 g unsalted butter
  • 3 tbsp olive oil for chimichurri
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 60 ml reserved sweet potato cooking water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Cover the sweet potatoes with water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil and simmer for 12–15 minutes until very tender. Reserve 60 ml cooking water, then drain. Mash with the butter and 1/4 teaspoon salt until smooth, adding reserved water a tablespoon at a time if needed. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Mix the parsley, minced garlic, red wine vinegar, olive oil and 1/4 teaspoon salt. Let stand while you cook the steak. Reserve a little of the oil from this mixture for the finishing drops.

  3. 03

    Place the sliced garlic and 1 tablespoon neutral oil in a cool skillet. Warm over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes until pale gold. Lift the chips onto kitchen paper before they turn brown.

  4. 04

    Pat the beef medallions dry and season with the remaining salt and pepper. Heat the remaining tablespoon oil in the skillet over medium-high heat. Sear for about 3–4 minutes per side, adjusting for thickness, until each center reaches 63°C / 145°F. Work in two batches if the pan is crowded.

  5. 05

    Rest the medallions on a clean warm plate for at least 3 minutes. Keep them whole for plating. Stir the warm sweet potato mash and loosen with a little reserved cooking water if needed.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Sweet potato purée

    Divide the sweet potato purée between two plates. Sweep each portion into a smooth arc from eight to two o’clock, slightly left of center.

  2. Step 2

    Seared steak

    Arrange three rested steak medallions in a slightly overlapping fan across each purée curve, with the browned surfaces facing up.

  3. Step 3

    Chimichurri

    Spoon chimichurri over the middle of each steak fan, leaving some of the browned surface visible.

  4. Step 4

    Chimichurri

    Use a teaspoon to place three small drops of the reserved chimichurri oil in the open space near four o’clock on each plate.

  5. Step 5

    Garlic chips

    Finish with the golden garlic chips and serve immediately. Follow the thermometer guidance in the cooking steps, regardless of the color shown in the image.
