Zucchini Fritters & Pea Purée

Crisp golden zucchini fritters on a swoosh of lemony pea purée, with a basil cherry tomato salad and cool lemon yogurt.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 medium zucchini, about 1 lb (450 g), coarsely grated
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/3 cup (40 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup (140 g) frozen peas
  • 1/2 cup (120 g) Greek yogurt
  • 1 cup (150 g) cherry tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 small bunch basil (about 15 leaves), 10 small leaves reserved
  • 6 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Toss the grated zucchini with 1/2 teaspoon salt and leave for 10 minutes. Wrap it in a clean towel and squeeze out as much liquid as you can.

  2. 02

    Boil the peas for 2–3 minutes. Drain, keeping a little of the water, and blend with 2 tablespoons oil, 1 tablespoon lemon juice, 3 basil leaves, a pinch of salt and 2–3 tablespoons of the cooking water until silky. Keep warm.

  3. 03

    Stir the zucchini with the egg, flour, the zest of half the lemon and the pepper. Heat 3 tablespoons oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Fry heaped tablespoons of the mixture, flattened slightly, for about 3 minutes per side until deep golden and cooked through. Drain on paper towels; you should have 6 fritters.

  4. 04

    Toss the tomatoes with 1 tablespoon oil, 1 teaspoon lemon juice, a pinch of salt and 2 torn basil leaves.

  5. 05

    Stir the yogurt with 1 teaspoon lemon juice and a pinch of salt.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Lemon pea purée

    Spoon a thick swoosh of pea purée in a curve from eight to one o’clock, leaving the lower right of the plate open.

  2. Step 2

    Zucchini fritters

    Lean 3 fritters in a loose overlapping line across the top of the swoosh, from eleven to one o’clock.

  3. Step 3

    Cherry tomato salad

    Spoon the tomato salad at two o’clock, tucked against the top fritter.

  4. Step 4

    Lemon yogurt

    Add a 2-tablespoon crescent of lemon yogurt at seven o’clock, beside the purée.

  5. Step 5

    Basil leaves

    Scatter 5 torn basil leaves over the top two fritters only, keeping their browned edges visible.
