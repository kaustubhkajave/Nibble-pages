Brioche French Toast

Golden brioche French toast on a warm berry compote, topped with whipped mascarpone, fresh berries and mint.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
20 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 thick slices brioche, halved into triangles
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup (80 ml) milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of ground cinnamon
  • 1 1/2 cups (200 g) mixed berries, fresh or frozen, plus a few fresh berries to finish
  • 2 tbsp sugar, divided
  • 1/2 cup (120 g) mascarpone
  • 2 tbsp heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 3 small mint leaves

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the mixed berries with 1 tablespoon sugar and a splash of water for 5–6 minutes until jammy. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Whisk the mascarpone with the cream and the remaining sugar until thick and smooth.

  3. 03

    Whisk the eggs, milk, vanilla and cinnamon in a shallow dish. Soak the brioche triangles for 20 seconds per side.

  4. 04

    Melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat and fry the brioche for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and set in the middle.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Berry compote

    Spoon a generous 4-inch (10 cm) pool of warm compote off center across the middle of the plate.

  2. Step 2

    Brioche French toast

    Overlap 2 French toast triangles in the center over the compote.

  3. Step 3

    Whipped mascarpone

    Add a quenelle of whipped mascarpone on the top triangle.

  4. Step 4

    Fresh berries

    Place 3 blueberries and 2 halved raspberries around the base of the toast.

  5. Step 5

    Mint leaves

    Finish with 3 small mint leaves on the mascarpone.
