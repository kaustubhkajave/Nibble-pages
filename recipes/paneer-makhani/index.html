Paneer & Makhani Sauce

Golden spiced paneer slabs on a glossy makhani tomato sauce, with quick-pickled red onion, mint and a mint herb oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Get the Nibble app
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 8 oz (225 g) paneer, cut into 3 thick slabs
  • 1 can (14 oz / 400 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 tsp garam masala, divided
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 lime
  • Small bunch of mint
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • Pinch of sugar

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Toss the sliced red onion with the lime juice and a pinch of salt. Set aside for 15 minutes.

  2. 02

    Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat and soften the chopped onion for 5 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger, 1 1/2 teaspoons garam masala and the chili powder and cook for 1 minute.

  3. 03

    Add the tomatoes, sugar and 1/2 teaspoon salt and simmer for 15 minutes. Blend until smooth, stir in the cream and keep warm over low heat.

  4. 04

    Rub the paneer with the remaining garam masala, the turmeric and a pinch of salt. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat and sear for 2 minutes per side until golden.

  5. 05

    Blend a handful of mint with the remaining 2 tablespoons oil and a pinch of salt until bright green. Keep 5 small leaves for garnish.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Makhani sauce

    Spoon about 4 tablespoons of makhani sauce into the center and spread it into a bold 5-inch (13 cm) circular pool.

  2. Step 2

    Spiced paneer

    Arrange the 3 paneer slabs overlapping at a slight angle across the top left of the pool.

  3. Step 3

    Pickled red onion

    Scatter 4 slices of pickled onion along the upper right edge of the paneer.

  4. Step 4

    Mint leaves

    Place 5 small mint leaves over the onion and paneer.

  5. Step 5

    Mint oil

    Drizzle 1 teaspoon of mint oil over the center slab of paneer.
