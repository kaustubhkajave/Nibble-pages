Garlic Shrimp & Polenta

Garlic butter shrimp on creamy polenta, with a charred red pepper and cilantro salsa.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 10 oz (300 g) large raw shrimp (about 10), peeled and deveined, tails on
  • 1/2 cup (80 g) quick-cook polenta
  • 2 cups (480 ml) water or low-sodium stock
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 lime
  • Small handful of cilantro, 10 small leaves reserved
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Char the bell pepper under a hot broiler or in a dry skillet, turning, for 8–10 minutes until blistered. Cool slightly, remove the stem and seeds and dice finely. Mix with 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro, the juice of half the lime and a pinch of salt.

  2. 02

    Bring the water and 1/2 teaspoon salt to a boil. Whisk in the polenta, lower the heat and stir for about 5 minutes until thick and creamy. Stir in 1 tablespoon butter and a third of the garlic. Cover to keep warm, loosening with a splash of hot water if it stiffens.

  3. 03

    Pat the shrimp dry and season with a pinch of salt and the pepper. Heat the oil in a skillet over high heat and sear the shrimp for 1–2 minutes per side, until pink and opaque all the way through.

  4. 04

    Lower the heat, add the remaining butter and garlic and spoon the foaming butter over the shrimp for 30 seconds. Squeeze over the remaining lime.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Creamy polenta

    Spoon half the polenta into the center of each plate and spread it into a 4-inch (10 cm) round with a shallow well.

  2. Step 2

    Garlic butter shrimp

    Arrange 3 shrimp in a tight overlapping crescent on the left half of the polenta, tails facing out.

  3. Step 3

    Garlic butter shrimp

    Set 2 more shrimp over the first three to build a rounded mound.

  4. Step 4

    Charred pepper salsa

    Spoon 2 tablespoons of salsa into the well beside the shrimp, letting it spill a little over the right edge.

  5. Step 5

    Cilantro leaves

    Finish with 5 cilantro leaves over the shrimp and salsa, keeping the rim clean.
