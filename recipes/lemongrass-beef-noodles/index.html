Lemongrass Beef & Noodles

Lemongrass-marinated seared beef over a nest of chilled rice noodles, with cucumber ribbons, pickled carrot and fresh herbs.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Get the Nibble app
Total
50 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340 g) flank or sirloin steak
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, tender inner part finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 4 oz (115 g) thin rice vermicelli
  • 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
  • 1/2 cucumber
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Small bunch each of mint and cilantro
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • Pinch of salt

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Pound or blend the lemongrass and garlic with the fish sauce and brown sugar into a rough paste. Coat the steak and marinate for 20 minutes.

  2. 02

    Toss the carrot with the rice vinegar, sugar and a pinch of salt and set aside to pickle while the beef marinates.

  3. 03

    Cook the noodles according to the package, rinse under cold water and drain very well. Shave the cucumber into ribbons with a peeler.

  4. 04

    Scrape most of the marinade off the steak. Heat the oil in a skillet over high heat and sear for 3–4 minutes per side until the thickest part reaches 145°F (63°C).

  5. 05

    Rest the steak for 5 minutes, then slice against the grain into strips.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Rice noodle nest

    Twist the noodles into a tight 4-inch (10 cm) round nest slightly left of center.

  2. Step 2

    Lemongrass beef

    Arrange 5 strips of beef overlapping in a diagonal line across the top of the nest.

  3. Step 3

    Cucumber ribbons

    Tuck 3 folded cucumber ribbons around the lower front curve of the nest, opposite the beef.

  4. Step 4

    Pickled carrot

    Place a neat cluster of pickled carrot across the right edge of the beef.

  5. Step 5

    Herbs

    Finish with 3 small sprigs of cilantro and mint on top of the beef and carrot.
