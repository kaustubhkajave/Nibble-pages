Duck Breast & Cherry Sauce

Crisp-skinned duck breast on a buttery parsnip purée and green beans, with a glossy cherry and shallot pan sauce.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
45 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 skin-on duck breasts, about 7 oz (200 g) each
  • 3 parsnips, about 10 oz (300 g), peeled and chopped
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 3 tbsp dark cherry preserves
  • 1 shallot, finely diced
  • 4 oz (115 g) French green beans, trimmed
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) stock or water
  • 1 tsp balsamic or red wine vinegar
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided, plus salt for the cooking water
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the parsnips in salted water for 12–15 minutes until soft. Drain and blend with 1 tablespoon butter and a splash of the water until smooth. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Score the duck skin in a crosshatch without cutting into the meat. Season with 1/2 teaspoon salt and the pepper. Lay skin-side down in a cold skillet and cook over medium-low heat for 12–15 minutes, pouring off fat as it collects, until the skin is crisp and golden.

  3. 03

    Turn the duck and cook for 3–4 minutes. Take it to 135°F (57°C) for pink duck as restaurants serve it, or to 165°F (74°C) to follow USDA poultry guidance. Rest for 8 minutes, then slice.

  4. 04

    Pour off all but 1 teaspoon of fat. Cook the shallot in the pan for 1 minute, then add the cherry preserves, stock and vinegar and simmer for 2–3 minutes until glossy.

  5. 05

    Blanch the green beans in boiling salted water for 3 minutes. Drain and toss with the remaining butter and a pinch of salt.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Parsnip purée

    Spoon about 3 tablespoons of parsnip purée left of center and spread it into a 4-inch (10 cm) round.

  2. Step 2

    Buttered green beans

    Lay 8 green beans side by side across the center of the purée.

  3. Step 3

    Pan-seared duck breast

    Fan 5 slices of duck over the beans, skin-side up.

  4. Step 4

    Cherry pan sauce

    Spoon 2 tablespoons of cherry sauce around the right base of the duck, letting it pool.

  5. Step 5

    Shallots

    Spoon a few shallots from the sauce over the duck slices.
