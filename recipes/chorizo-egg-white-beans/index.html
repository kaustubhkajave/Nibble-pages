Chorizo, Egg & White Beans

Smoky tomato white beans with crisp chorizo coins, a fried egg and a pile of crunchy paprika beans.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
25 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 4 oz (115 g) cured Spanish chorizo, sliced into thin coins
  • 1 can (15 oz / 400 g) cannellini beans, drained and patted dry
  • 1/2 can (7 oz / 200 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika, divided
  • 2 tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2–3 tbsp water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Fry the chorizo in a dry skillet over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until crisp. Lift out the chorizo, keeping the red oil in the pan.

  2. 02

    Crisp 1/4 cup of the beans in 1 tablespoon of the chorizo oil with a pinch of paprika over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, for 5–6 minutes. Set aside.

  3. 03

    Lower the heat. Sizzle the garlic in the remaining chorizo oil for 1 minute, then add the rest of the paprika, the tomatoes, the remaining beans and the water. Simmer for 8–10 minutes until thick. Season with the salt.

  4. 04

    Fry the eggs in the olive oil over medium heat until the whites are fully set and the edges are crisp; cook longer if you prefer a firm yolk.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Smoky tomato beans

    Spoon half the tomato beans into a 6-inch (15 cm) oval slightly below the center of each plate.

  2. Step 2

    Crisp chorizo

    Overlap the chorizo coins in an arc along the upper right edge of the beans.

  3. Step 3

    Fried egg

    Set one fried egg on the upper left of the beans, yolk toward the center.

  4. Step 4

    Crispy paprika beans

    Pile 2 tablespoons of crispy beans in a compact cluster at the lower right.

  5. Step 5

    Parsley

    Sprinkle 1 teaspoon of parsley over the egg and the crispy beans, keeping the rim clean.
