Tomato Spaghetti & Parmesan

Tomato spaghetti twirled into a tall nest, with golden garlic breadcrumbs, a curved Parmesan crisp and fresh basil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 7 oz (200 g) spaghetti
  • 1 can (14 oz / 400 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 4 garlic cloves, 3 sliced and 1 minced
  • 1/3 cup (25 g) dry breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup (40 g) finely grated Parmesan, divided
  • 8 basil leaves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus salt for the pasta water
  • Pinch of chili flakes (optional)

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Make 2 small mounds of Parmesan, about 2 tablespoons each, on a lined baking sheet. Bake for 5–7 minutes until golden, then drape over a rolling pin to curve as they cool.

  2. 02

    Sizzle the sliced garlic in 2 tablespoons oil over medium heat for 1 minute. Add the tomatoes, the salt and the chili flakes, if using, and simmer for 15 minutes until thickened.

  3. 03

    Toast the breadcrumbs in the remaining oil with the minced garlic over medium heat for 2–3 minutes, stirring, until golden.

  4. 04

    Cook the spaghetti in well-salted water for 1 minute less than the package directions. Set aside 3/4 cup (180 ml) of the sauce for plating, then toss the pasta in the rest with a splash of pasta water and the remaining Parmesan.

  5. 05

    Twirl each portion with tongs inside a ladle to form a tight nest.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Tomato sauce

    Spoon half the reserved sauce into a tall oval in the center of each plate.

  2. Step 2

    Spaghetti nest

    Set one spaghetti nest upright in the center of the sauce.

  3. Step 3

    Parmesan crisp

    Lean one Parmesan crisp against the nest at the upper right.

  4. Step 4

    Garlic breadcrumbs

    Spoon 2 tablespoons of breadcrumbs in a crescent along the lower left of the sauce.

  5. Step 5

    Basil ribbons

    Slice 4 basil leaves into ribbons and lay them loosely across the top of the nest.
