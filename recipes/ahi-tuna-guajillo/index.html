Ahi Tuna & Guajillo

Seared ahi tuna slices on a smoky guajillo chili sauce, with a bright mango-avocado relish, cilantro and olive oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Get the Nibble app
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 sushi-grade ahi tuna steaks (5 oz / 140 g each)
  • 3 dried guajillo chilies, stemmed and seeded
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 mango, diced
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1/2 small red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 lime
  • Small bunch of cilantro
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Toast the guajillos in a dry pan for 30 seconds until fragrant, then soak in hot water for 10 minutes.

  2. 02

    Drain the chilies and blend with the garlic, stock and 1/4 teaspoon salt until smooth. Simmer for 3 minutes and keep warm.

  3. 03

    Gently mix the mango, avocado, onion, lime juice, a little chopped cilantro and a pinch of salt.

  4. 04

    Season the tuna with the remaining salt and the pepper. Sear in the neutral oil over very high heat for 45–60 seconds per side for a rare center, as restaurants serve it (use sushi-grade tuna only), or cook longer to 145°F (63°C) for fully cooked fish. Slice thickly.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Guajillo sauce

    Spoon about 3 tablespoons of guajillo sauce left of center and sweep it into a smooth 5-inch (13 cm) arc.

  2. Step 2

    Seared ahi tuna

    Fan 4 thick slices of tuna across the sauce, showing the cut faces.

  3. Step 3

    Mango-avocado relish

    Spoon 2 tablespoons of relish along the top edge of the tuna slices.

  4. Step 4

    Cilantro and olive oil

    Place 3 cilantro leaves over the relish.

  5. Step 5

    Cilantro and olive oil

    Drizzle 1 teaspoon of olive oil over the tuna slices only.
