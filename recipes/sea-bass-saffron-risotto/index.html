Sea Bass & Saffron Risotto

Crisp-skinned sea bass on golden saffron risotto, with blistered cherry tomatoes and basil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 skin-on sea bass fillets, about 130 g each, pin bones removed
  • 160 g arborio rice
  • 1 small shallot, finely chopped
  • 1/8 tsp saffron threads
  • 800 ml hot low-sodium chicken or vegetable stock
  • 40 g unsalted butter, divided
  • 30 g Parmesan, finely grated
  • 14 cherry tomatoes
  • Small basil leaves to garnish
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Crumble the saffron into 3 tablespoons of the hot stock and leave to steep. Keep the rest of the stock hot over low heat.

  2. 02

    Melt 20 g butter in a wide saucepan over medium heat and cook the shallot for 2–3 minutes until soft. Add the rice and stir for 1–2 minutes until the edges turn translucent. Add the stock a ladle at a time, stirring often and waiting until each addition is absorbed, for 16–18 minutes until the rice is tender with a slight bite. Stir in the saffron liquid halfway through.

  3. 03

    Take the risotto off the heat and beat in the remaining butter and the Parmesan. Season with a pinch of salt. Keep it slightly firmer than usual so it holds a round shape.

  4. 04

    While the risotto cooks, heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the tomatoes for 4–5 minutes, shaking the pan, until blistered. Season with a pinch of salt and set aside.

  5. 05

    Pat the sea bass very dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Wipe out the skillet and heat the remaining oil over medium-high heat. Lay the fillets skin-side down and press gently for 15 seconds to stop them curling. Cook for 3–4 minutes until the skin is crisp, then flip and cook for 30–60 seconds until the center reaches 63°C / 145°F.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Saffron risotto

    Set a 10 cm (4 in) ring in the center of each plate, fill it with risotto and level the top, then lift the ring away.

  2. Step 2

    Crispy sea bass

    Lay a sea bass fillet skin-side up diagonally across the risotto.

  3. Step 3

    Blistered tomatoes

    Nestle a cluster of blistered tomatoes against the fish at two o’clock.

  4. Step 4

    Blistered tomatoes

    Add a smaller cluster of tomatoes at eight o’clock to balance the plate.

  5. Step 5

    Basil

    Tuck small basil leaves between the fish and the tomatoes and serve at once.
