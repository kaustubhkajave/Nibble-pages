Burrata & Charred Zucchini

Torn burrata on a warm garlic white bean purée, with charred zucchini rounds, blistered tomatoes and basil oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz / 400 g) cannellini beans, drained
  • 1 ball (4 oz / 115 g) burrata
  • 1 medium zucchini, cut into 1/2-inch (1 cm) rounds
  • 10 cherry tomatoes
  • Large handful of basil (about 1 cup / 20 g), 8 small leaves reserved
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • 7 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Take the burrata out of the fridge 15 minutes before plating.

  2. 02

    Warm the beans with the garlic, 3 tablespoons oil and the water for 5 minutes. Blend until silky with 1 teaspoon lemon juice and 1/4 teaspoon salt. Keep warm.

  3. 03

    Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a skillet over high heat. Sear the zucchini rounds for about 2 minutes per side until charred, seasoning with a pinch of salt and the pepper. Blister the tomatoes in the same pan for 3 minutes.

  4. 04

    Blend the basil with the remaining oil and a pinch of salt, then strain.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Garlic white bean purée

    Spoon half the bean purée into a smooth 5-inch (13 cm) circle in the center of each plate.

  2. Step 2

    Charred zucchini

    Arrange 4 zucchini rounds overlapping in an arc along the left edge of the purée.

  3. Step 3

    Burrata

    Tear half the burrata and place it on the center right of the purée.

  4. Step 4

    Blistered tomatoes

    Spoon 5 blistered tomatoes into the curve between the zucchini and burrata.

  5. Step 5

    Basil oil and leaves

    Drizzle 1 tablespoon of basil oil around the edge of the purée and top with 4 small basil leaves.
