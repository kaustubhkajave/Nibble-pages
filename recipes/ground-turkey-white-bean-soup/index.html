Turkey & Bean Soup

A cozy tomato broth with browned turkey, creamy white beans, carrots and tender kale.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Total
30 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 450 g ground turkey
  • 1 can (425 g) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 60 g kale, stems removed and leaves chopped
  • 2 medium carrots, diced small
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 litre chicken broth
  • 1 can (400 g) diced tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the turkey, salt and pepper. Break it into small pieces and cook for 6–8 minutes, until it reaches 74°C / 165°F.

  2. 02

    Add the onion and carrots. Cook over medium heat for 5 minutes, stirring. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.

  3. 03

    Pour in the broth and tomatoes with their juices, then add the beans. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 10 minutes.

  4. 04

    Stir in the kale and simmer for 3–5 minutes, until the kale and carrots are tender. Taste, adjust the seasoning and divide between four bowls.
