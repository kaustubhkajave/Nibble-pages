Spanish Tortilla & Aioli

A thick wedge of slow-cooked Spanish potato tortilla with garlic aioli and a bright herb-tossed cherry tomato salad.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
45 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 5 eggs
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) olive oil, plus 1 tbsp
  • 1/4 cup (60 g) mayonnaise
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated
  • 1 cup (150 g) cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 lemon
  • Small bunch of parsley
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Cook the potatoes and onion in the 1/2 cup olive oil over medium-low heat for about 20 minutes, turning occasionally, until soft but not brown. Drain, keeping the oil.

  2. 02

    Beat the eggs with 1/2 teaspoon salt, fold in the potatoes and onion and rest for 5 minutes.

  3. 03

    Heat 1 tablespoon of the reserved oil in a small nonstick skillet over low heat. Add the mixture and cook for 6–8 minutes until the edges set. Flip onto a plate, slide back into the pan and cook for 3–4 minutes more until set in the middle.

  4. 04

    Stir the garlic and a squeeze of lemon into the mayonnaise. Toss the tomatoes with the remaining 1 tablespoon oil, the remaining salt and chopped parsley, keeping a few whole leaves.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Garlic aioli

    Spoon 2 tablespoons of aioli slightly left of center and spread it into a smooth 3-inch (8 cm) pool.

  2. Step 2

    Spanish tortilla

    Set a thick tortilla wedge overlapping the right side of the aioli, point toward twelve o’clock.

  3. Step 3

    Tomato-parsley salad

    Spoon 3 tablespoons of tomato salad along the lower right edge of the wedge.

  4. Step 4

    Tomato-parsley salad

    Spoon 1 tablespoon of tomato salad along the top ridge of the wedge.

  5. Step 5

    Parsley leaves

    Finish with 4 whole parsley leaves over the tomatoes and wipe the rim.
