Sausage & Cannellini Purée

A seared Italian sausage over silky garlic cannellini purée, with crisp kale, blistered cherry tomatoes and lemon zest.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Get the Nibble app
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 large Italian sausage links, about 6 oz (170 g) each
  • 1 (15-oz) can (425 g) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 6 cherry tomatoes
  • 4 lacinato kale leaves, stems removed, torn into large pieces
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/3 cup (80 ml) warm water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Warm the beans and garlic in 1 tbsp olive oil over low heat for 5 minutes without browning. Blend with the warm water, salt and pepper until completely smooth, adding a splash more water if needed. Keep warm and covered.

  2. 02

    Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the sausages for 12–14 minutes, turning every few minutes, until deep golden all over and they reach 160°F (71°C). Rest on a warm plate.

  3. 03

    Add the tomatoes to the pan drippings and cook for 3–4 minutes, shaking the pan, until blistered and just starting to burst. Squeeze over a little lemon juice and spoon onto a plate.

  4. 04

    Add the remaining oil, turn the heat to medium-high and fry the kale in a single layer for 1–2 minutes until crisp at the edges and bright green. Drain on paper towel and salt lightly.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Garlic cannellini purée

    Spoon half the purée onto the center of each warm plate and smooth it with the back of the spoon into a thick oval.

  2. Step 2

    Seared sausage

    Lay one sausage diagonally across the middle of each purée oval, browned side up.

  3. Step 3

    Crisp kale

    Tuck a cluster of crisp kale against the upper end of the sausage, at the top left of the purée.

  4. Step 4

    Blistered tomatoes

    Nestle three blistered tomatoes into the purée along the lower right, close to the sausage.

  5. Step 5

    Lemon zest

    Finish by grating fine lemon zest over the sausage.
