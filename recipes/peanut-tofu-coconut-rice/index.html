Peanut Tofu & Coconut Rice

Crisp seared tofu over coconut jasmine rice, with charred scallions, a glossy peanut-soy sauce and a spoonful of chili crisp.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 400 g extra-firm tofu, pressed for 10 minutes
  • 200 g jasmine rice, rinsed
  • 250 ml canned coconut milk
  • 120 ml water, plus 2–3 tbsp for the sauce
  • 4 scallions, roots trimmed
  • 2 tbsp creamy peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tsp chili crisp
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Combine the rice, coconut milk, 120 ml water and the salt in a saucepan. Bring to a simmer, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 minutes. Rest off the heat, covered, for 10 minutes, then fluff.

  2. 02

    Whisk the peanut butter and soy sauce with 2–3 tablespoons warm water until glossy and pourable.

  3. 03

    Cut the tofu into six rectangles about 2 cm thick, pat dry and toss in the cornstarch. Heat 1 1/2 tablespoons oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and crisp.

  4. 04

    Pat the scallions completely dry. Add the remaining oil to the pan and cook them whole for 2–3 minutes, turning, until charred in spots and tender. Wet scallions spatter in hot oil.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Coconut rice

    Pack half the rice into a cup or 10 cm ring and turn it out as a neat disc on the lower left of each plate.

  2. Step 2

    Crispy tofu

    Shingle three tofu pieces diagonally across the top half of the rice, overlapping slightly.

  3. Step 3

    Charred scallions

    Lay two charred scallions along the lower edge of the rice, resting against the lowest tofu piece.

  4. Step 4

    Peanut-soy sauce

    Spoon a tablespoon of peanut sauce in a clean arc around the upper edge of the rice.

  5. Step 5

    Chili crisp

    Spoon a teaspoon of chili crisp over the top tofu piece.
