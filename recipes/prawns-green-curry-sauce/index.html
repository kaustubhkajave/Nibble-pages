Prawns & Green Curry Sauce

Seared jumbo prawns on a glossy Thai green curry coconut sauce, with crispy shallots, red chili and Thai basil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 10 oz (300 g) jumbo raw prawns (about 6), peeled and deveined, tails on
  • 1 can (13.5 oz / 400 ml) full-fat coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp Thai green curry paste
  • 3 shallots, thinly sliced
  • 1 red Thai chili, thinly sliced
  • 8 Thai basil leaves
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) neutral oil, divided
  • Pinch of salt

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat 3 tablespoons oil in a small pan over medium heat. Fry the shallots, stirring often, for 6–8 minutes until golden. Lift onto a paper towel and salt lightly, keeping the oil.

  2. 02

    Fry the curry paste in 1 tablespoon of the shallot oil for 1 minute. Add the coconut milk and simmer for 8–10 minutes until thick and glossy. Season with the fish sauce, sugar and 1 tablespoon lime juice. Keep warm.

  3. 03

    Pat the prawns dry. Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over high heat and sear for 1–2 minutes per side, until pink and opaque all the way through.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Green curry sauce

    Spoon a thick 4-inch (10 cm) arc of curry sauce across the upper left of each plate.

  2. Step 2

    Seared prawns

    Nestle 3 prawns close together along the curve of the sauce, tails up.

  3. Step 3

    Crispy shallots

    Top the prawns with 2 tablespoons of crispy shallots.

  4. Step 4

    Red chili

    Scatter 6 thin chili rings over the prawns and shallots.

  5. Step 5

    Thai basil

    Tuck 4 Thai basil leaves around the prawns.
