White Fish & Corn Purée

Butter-basted white fish on a silky sweet corn purée, with blistered cherry tomatoes, basil oil and fresh basil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
25 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 white fish fillets, such as cod, halibut or hake, about 5 oz (150 g) each
  • 1 1/2 cups (200 g) frozen corn
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) water
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 8 cherry tomatoes
  • Handful of basil (about 1/2 cup / 10 g), 6 small leaves reserved
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the corn with the garlic in the water for 5 minutes. Blend with 1 tablespoon butter and a pinch of salt until very smooth; strain for extra silkiness. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Blend the basil with 3 tablespoons oil and a pinch of salt, then strain.

  3. 03

    Blister the tomatoes in a hot skillet with a little oil for 3–4 minutes. Season with a pinch of salt and set aside.

  4. 04

    Pat the fish dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat the remaining oil in the skillet over medium-high heat and sear the fish for 3–4 minutes until golden underneath.

  5. 05

    Turn the fish, add the remaining butter and spoon it over the fish for 1–2 minutes, until the flesh is opaque and flakes easily, or reaches 145°F (63°C). Finish with a squeeze of lemon.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Sweet corn purée

    Spoon about 3 tablespoons of corn purée into the center of each plate and push it into a thick, smooth circle with the back of the spoon.

  2. Step 2

    Butter-basted white fish

    Set the fish across the upper center of the purée at a slight diagonal, golden side up.

  3. Step 3

    Blistered tomatoes

    Nestle 4 blistered tomatoes along the left edge of the fish.

  4. Step 4

    Basil oil

    Spoon 1 teaspoon of basil oil in drops around the purée.

  5. Step 5

    Basil leaves

    Top the fish with 3 small torn basil leaves.
