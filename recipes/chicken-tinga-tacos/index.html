Chicken Tinga Tacos

Smoky shredded chicken simmered in chipotle and tomato, piled into warm corn tortillas with avocado and cilantro.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
30 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) boneless skinless chicken breasts, cut into thin strips
  • 1 yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2–3 chipotle peppers in adobo, chopped, plus 1 tbsp adobo sauce
  • 1 (14.5-oz) can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) water
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 2 avocados, sliced
  • 1 small bunch cilantro, chopped
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Season the chicken with half the salt and the pepper. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and brown the chicken for 4 minutes, turning once.

  2. 02

    Add the onion and cook for 3 minutes until softened, then stir in the garlic for 30 seconds.

  3. 03

    Stir in the chipotles, adobo sauce, crushed tomatoes, water and remaining salt. Simmer for 8–10 minutes until the sauce thickens and the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C). Shred the chicken in the pan with two forks.

  4. 04

    Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet for about 30 seconds per side and wrap in a towel.

  5. 05

    Fill the tortillas with the chicken tinga and top with avocado and cilantro.
