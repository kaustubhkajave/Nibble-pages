Sea Bass & Red Pepper Sauce

Crisp-skinned sea bass on lemony cannellini beans and a roasted red pepper sauce, with blistered tomatoes and zucchini ribbons.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 skin-on sea bass fillets, about 5 oz (150 g) each
  • 2 red bell peppers (or 1 cup / 150 g jarred roasted peppers)
  • 1 can (15 oz / 400 g) cannellini beans, drained
  • 1 small zucchini
  • 8 cherry tomatoes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • 5 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 2–3 tbsp water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C). Roast the bell peppers with 2 unpeeled garlic cloves for about 20 minutes until blistered, or use jarred peppers. Peel and seed the peppers, squeeze the garlic from its skin, and blend with 2 tablespoons oil, 1 teaspoon lemon juice and 1/4 teaspoon salt until smooth.

  2. 02

    Warm the beans in 1 tablespoon oil with the last garlic clove, minced, the zest of the lemon, a pinch of salt and 2–3 tablespoons water for 4–5 minutes.

  3. 03

    Blister the tomatoes in a hot skillet with a little oil for 3–4 minutes. Season with a pinch of salt.

  4. 04

    Shave the zucchini into ribbons with a peeler and dress with 1 teaspoon lemon juice, a little oil and a pinch of salt.

  5. 05

    Score the fish skin lightly, pat dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook skin-side down for 3–4 minutes until crisp, then turn for about 1 minute until the flesh is opaque and flakes easily, or reaches 145°F (63°C).

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Red pepper sauce

    Spoon a long swoosh of red pepper sauce from eight o’clock through the center toward one o’clock, leaving the lower right clear.

  2. Step 2

    Lemony cannellini beans

    Spoon a generous mound of beans over the sauce at eleven o’clock.

  3. Step 3

    Crisp-skinned sea bass

    Set one fillet, skin-side up, diagonally over the beans and sauce.

  4. Step 4

    Blistered tomatoes

    Place 4 blistered tomatoes in a loose cluster at eight o’clock beside the fish.

  5. Step 5

    Zucchini ribbons

    Drape 3 zucchini ribbons across the top of the fish at eleven o’clock.
