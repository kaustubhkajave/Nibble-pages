Chicken & Spinach Purée

Golden chicken cutlets over a silky spinach purée, with blistered tomatoes, crisp garlic chips and chives.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
30 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts, about 350 g total, each sliced horizontally into 2 cutlets
  • 200 g baby spinach
  • 10 cherry tomatoes
  • 4 garlic cloves, 1 minced and 3 thinly sliced
  • 30 g unsalted butter, divided
  • 45 ml heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped chives
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Place the sliced garlic and 1 tablespoon oil in a cool skillet. Warm over medium-low heat for 2–3 minutes until pale gold, then lift the chips onto kitchen paper. Keep the oil in the pan.

  2. 02

    Blanch the spinach in boiling water for 30 seconds, cool it in cold water and squeeze out most of the water. Melt 15 g butter in a small saucepan, cook the minced garlic for 1 minute, then add the cream and bring to a simmer. Blend the spinach with the garlic cream and 1/4 teaspoon salt until smooth. Rewarm gently just before plating, as long heating dulls the color.

  3. 03

    Heat the garlic oil in the skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the tomatoes for 4–5 minutes, shaking the pan, until the skins blister. Season with a pinch of salt and set aside.

  4. 04

    Pat the chicken dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Add the remaining oil and 15 g butter to the skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the cutlets for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and the thickest part reaches 74°C / 165°F. Rest for 2 minutes.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Spinach purée

    Spoon half the spinach purée into the center of each plate and spread it into a round about 10 cm (4 in) across.

  2. Step 2

    Seared chicken

    Lay two chicken cutlets, slightly overlapping, over the left half of the purée.

  3. Step 3

    Blistered tomatoes

    Spoon five blistered tomatoes onto the right half of the purée.

  4. Step 4

    Garlic chips

    Scatter the garlic chips over the chicken and tomatoes.

  5. Step 5

    Chives

    Sprinkle the chives over the chicken and purée and serve.
