Potato Cakes & Mint Yogurt

Crisp spiced potato and pea cakes on a bright mint-cilantro yogurt, with charred peas and pickled red onion.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 1/2 cup (70 g) frozen peas, thawed, plus 1/4 cup (35 g) to char
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch or all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup (120 g) Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cup (10 g) mint leaves, 6 small leaves reserved
  • 1/2 cup (10 g) cilantro leaves
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1 tsp salt, divided, plus salt for the cooking water

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Boil the potatoes in salted water for 12–15 minutes until tender. Drain, leave to steam dry for a few minutes, then mash.

  2. 02

    Toss the onion with the juice of half the lemon and a pinch of salt.

  3. 03

    Blend the yogurt, mint, cilantro, 1 teaspoon lemon juice and a pinch of salt until bright green.

  4. 04

    Mix the mash with the thawed peas, garam masala, cornstarch and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Shape into 4 cakes about 1/2 inch (1 cm) thick.

  5. 05

    Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat and fry the cakes for 3–4 minutes per side until crisp and golden. Char the extra peas in a dry hot pan for 2 minutes and season with a pinch of salt.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Mint-cilantro yogurt

    Spoon about 3 tablespoons of mint yogurt onto the upper center of each plate and smooth it into a thick round pool.

  2. Step 2

    Spiced potato cakes

    Overlap 2 potato cakes across the center of the yogurt, keeping their crisp edges visible.

  3. Step 3

    Charred peas

    Scatter 2 tablespoons of charred peas along the bottom edge of the cakes.

  4. Step 4

    Pickled red onion

    Pile a small mound of pickled onion on the top cake.

  5. Step 5

    Mint leaves

    Finish with 3 small mint leaves on the onion.
