Lamb & Smoky Eggplant

Seared lamb over smoky roasted eggplant purée and a red tomato-pepper sauce, with garlic yogurt and mint oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
55 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340 g) lamb sirloin or loin, about 1 1/2 inches (4 cm) thick
  • 1 large eggplant
  • 3 roma tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, divided
  • 1/3 cup (80 g) Greek yogurt
  • 1 small bunch fresh mint
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C). Prick the eggplant and roast for 35–40 minutes until collapsed and soft. Scoop out the flesh and blend with 1 grated garlic clove, 1 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt until smooth.

  2. 02

    Meanwhile, cook the tomatoes, pepper and 1 sliced garlic clove in 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat for 12–15 minutes until soft. Blend until smooth and season with salt.

  3. 03

    Stir the remaining grated garlic and a pinch of salt into the yogurt. Blend most of the mint with 1 tbsp olive oil and strain.

  4. 04

    Season the lamb with the remaining salt and the pepper. Sear in the remaining oil over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes per side, until it reaches 130–135°F (54–57°C) for medium-rare or 145°F (63°C) for medium. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice thickly.

  5. 05

    Pool the tomato sauce on each plate, spoon a mound of eggplant purée into its upper half, overlap the lamb slices across it, add three dots of garlic yogurt and finish with drops of mint oil.
