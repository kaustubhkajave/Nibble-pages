Herb Omelet & Glazed Carrots

A soft spinach and herb omelet with butter-glazed carrots, a crisp tomato-cucumber salad and mustard yogurt.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
25 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 5 large eggs
  • 1 cup (30 g) baby spinach, chopped
  • 3 tbsp chopped fresh herbs, such as parsley, chives or dill
  • 3 medium carrots, cut into thin sticks
  • 2 tbsp (30 g) unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 cucumber, diced
  • 1/3 cup (80 g) Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Melt 1 tbsp butter in a skillet over medium heat. Add the carrots, 2 tbsp water and a pinch of salt. Cover and cook for 5 minutes, then uncover and cook for 3–4 minutes more until tender and glossy. Set aside.

  2. 02

    Toss the tomatoes and cucumber with a pinch of salt. Stir the mustard into the yogurt.

  3. 03

    Whisk the eggs with the spinach, herbs, remaining salt and the pepper.

  4. 04

    Wipe the skillet, melt the remaining butter over medium-low heat and pour in the eggs. Draw the set edges toward the center until there is no runny egg left, about 3–4 minutes, then fold in half.

  5. 05

    Cut the omelet in two and plate with the glazed carrots, the salad and a spoonful of mustard yogurt.
