Sweet Potato & Rajma

Caramelized sweet potato rounds over a silky spiced kidney bean and tomato sauce, with charred peppers, spinach and pink onion.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
45 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, about 1 lb (450 g), cut into 3/4-inch (2 cm) rounds
  • 1 (15-oz) can (425 g) red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 3 roma tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into strips
  • 1 red onion, half chopped and half very thinly sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 large handfuls fresh spinach
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tsp garam masala or curry powder
  • 3 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) water
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Toss the sliced onion with the juice of the lime and a pinch of salt. Set aside to turn pink.

  2. 02

    Cook the chopped onion in 1 tbsp oil over medium heat for 4 minutes. Add half the garlic and ginger, the garam masala and tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes. Add half the beans and the water and simmer for 5 minutes, then blend until silky. Season with salt.

  3. 03

    Heat 1 1/2 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the sweet potato rounds for 5–6 minutes per side, covered for the first side, until tender and caramelized. Season with salt.

  4. 04

    In the same pan, char the pepper strips over high heat for 3 minutes. Add the remaining beans, garlic and ginger for 1 minute, then set aside. Wilt the spinach in the remaining oil for 30 seconds.

  5. 05

    Spoon the bean sauce in an oval on each plate. Overlap the sweet potatoes across it, add the pepper and bean mixture at the lower right and the spinach on the left, then top with a few pink onion ribbons.
