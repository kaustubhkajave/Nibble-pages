Halloumi & Minted Peas

Golden seared halloumi over a bright minted pea purée, with blistered cherry tomatoes and lemon zest.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
25 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 250 g halloumi, cut into 6 slices about 1 cm thick
  • 300 g frozen peas
  • 15 g fresh mint, with 12 small leaves set aside
  • 10 cherry tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 30 g unsalted butter
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/4 tsp salt, plus more for the pea water
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Boil the peas in lightly salted water for 3 minutes until tender. Reserve 60 ml of the cooking water, then drain. Blend the peas with the butter, the mint (except the reserved leaves), 1 tablespoon lemon juice, the salt and pepper, adding reserved water a tablespoon at a time until smooth and spoonable. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tomatoes and cook for 4–5 minutes, shaking the pan, until the skins blister. Add the garlic for the last minute and cook until fragrant but not brown. Transfer to a bowl.

  3. 03

    Pat the halloumi dry. Wipe out the skillet and heat the remaining oil over medium-high heat. Sear the halloumi for 1–2 minutes per side until deep golden. Plate straight away, as halloumi firms up as it cools.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Pea purée

    Spoon half the pea purée across the lower center of each plate and swipe it into a thick curve, leaving the top of the plate clear.

  2. Step 2

    Seared halloumi

    Lay three halloumi slices overlapping in a diagonal line across the middle of the purée.

  3. Step 3

    Blistered tomatoes

    Arrange five blistered tomatoes along the right side of the halloumi, resting against the slices.

  4. Step 4

    Mint

    Scatter torn mint leaves over the halloumi and tomatoes.

  5. Step 5

    Lemon zest

    Finish with a pinch of lemon zest over the halloumi and serve immediately.
