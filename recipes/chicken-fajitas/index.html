Chicken Fajitas

A tray of smoky chicken, sweet peppers and onions, tucked into warm tortillas with avocado and lime.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Total
30 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 680 g boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into thin strips
  • 2 red bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 yellow onion, sliced
  • 8 small flour tortillas
  • 2 limes
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 15 g fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat the oven to 220°C / 425°F. On a large rimmed baking sheet, toss the chicken, peppers and onion with the oil, paprika, cumin, salt, pepper and juice of one lime. Spread out evenly; use two trays if crowded.

  2. 02

    Roast for 18–22 minutes, tossing halfway through, until the chicken reaches 74°C / 165°F in the thickest strips and the vegetables are tender.

  3. 03

    Wrap the tortillas in foil and warm in the oven during the final 5 minutes. Slice the avocado and cut the second lime into wedges.

  4. 04

    Divide the chicken and vegetables among the warm tortillas. Add avocado and cilantro, then serve with lime wedges.
