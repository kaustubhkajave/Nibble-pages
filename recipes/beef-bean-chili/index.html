Beef & Bean Chili

A rich, spoonable chili of browned beef, kidney beans and sweet peppers in a smoky tomato base.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
40 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) ground beef
  • 2 (15-oz) cans (425 g each) red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 (28-oz) can (800 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) water
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Heat the oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the beef and cook for 6–8 minutes, breaking it up, until browned with no pink left and it reaches 160°F (71°C).

  2. 02

    Add the onion, bell pepper and jalapeño. Cook for 4–5 minutes until softened. Stir in the garlic, chili powder and cumin and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

  3. 03

    Stir in the crushed tomatoes, kidney beans, water, salt and pepper. Bring to a simmer, then lower the heat and cook uncovered for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick.

  4. 04

    Taste and adjust the salt. Ladle into bowls and serve hot.
