Lime Fish Tacos

Golden fish, crunchy red cabbage and a tangy lime crema in warm corn tortillas.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Total
25 minutes
Serves
4

Ingredients

  • 450 g skinless cod, cut into thick strips
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 150 g shredded red cabbage
  • 2 limes
  • 120 g sour cream
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce, plus more to taste
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Mix the sour cream, hot sauce and juice of one lime with a pinch of the salt. Toss half with the cabbage. Keep the rest for drizzling. Cut the second lime into wedges.

  2. 02

    Pat the fish dry and season with the remaining salt and pepper. Heat the oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the fish for about 3–4 minutes per side, turning gently, until it reaches 63°C / 145°F. Work in batches if needed.

  3. 03

    Warm the tortillas in a separate dry skillet for about 30 seconds per side. Stack inside a clean towel to keep them soft.

  4. 04

    Divide the fish among the tortillas. Top with cabbage slaw, drizzle with the remaining crema and serve with lime wedges.
