Aji Verde Chicken & Potatoes

Sliced seared chicken over a creamy jalapeño-cilantro aji verde, with crisp potato rounds and quick pickled red onion.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts, about 8 oz (225 g) each
  • 12 oz (340 g) Yukon Gold potatoes, sliced into 1/2-inch (1.25 cm) rounds
  • 1–2 jalapeños, seeded and chopped
  • 1 large bunch cilantro, a few leaves reserved
  • 1/3 cup (80 g) mayonnaise
  • 2 limes
  • 1/2 red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided, plus salt for boiling
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Toss the onion with the juice of one lime and a pinch of salt and set aside for 15 minutes. Blend the jalapeños, cilantro, mayonnaise, juice of the second lime, water and a pinch of salt until smooth.

  2. 02

    Simmer the potato rounds in salted water for 6–8 minutes until just tender, then drain well.

  3. 03

    Season the chicken with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium heat and cook for 5–7 minutes per side until golden and it reaches 165°F (74°C). Rest for 5 minutes.

  4. 04

    Add the remaining oil and fry the potatoes for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and crisp.

  5. 05

    Sweep the aji verde into a circle on each plate. Arch the potatoes along the left side, fan the sliced chicken across the center, then top with a pinch of pickled onion and a few cilantro leaves.
