Lamb Kofta & Turmeric Yogurt

Spiced lamb kofta seared until crisp, on golden turmeric yogurt with sumac onions, mint oil and pomegranate.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340 g) ground lamb
  • 3/4 cup (180 g) Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 red onion
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • Small bunch of mint (about 1/2 cup / 10 g)
  • 2 tbsp pomegranate seeds
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Thinly slice half the onion and toss with the sumac, 1 teaspoon lemon juice and a pinch of salt.

  2. 02

    Grate the other half of the onion and squeeze out its juice. Mix with the lamb, 1 grated garlic clove, the cumin, 1 tablespoon chopped mint, 3/4 teaspoon salt and the pepper. Shape into 6 oval kofta.

  3. 03

    Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the kofta, turning, for 8–10 minutes until browned all over and 160°F (71°C) inside.

  4. 04

    Stir the yogurt with the turmeric, the remaining garlic clove (grated), 1 teaspoon lemon juice and a pinch of salt.

  5. 05

    Blend the remaining mint with the remaining oil and a pinch of salt, then strain.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Turmeric yogurt

    Spoon about 4 tablespoons of turmeric yogurt on the left of each plate and smooth it into a glossy 5-inch (13 cm) round.

  2. Step 2

    Lamb kofta

    Lay 3 kofta on the yogurt, slightly overlapping in a diagonal line.

  3. Step 3

    Sumac onions

    Place a loose tangle of sumac onions beside the kofta at about five o’clock.

  4. Step 4

    Mint oil

    Drizzle 1 teaspoon of mint oil over the kofta and the yogurt.

  5. Step 5

    Pomegranate seeds

    Scatter 1 tablespoon of pomegranate seeds over the kofta and onions.
