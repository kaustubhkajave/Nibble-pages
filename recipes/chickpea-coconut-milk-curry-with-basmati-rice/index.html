Coconut Chickpea Curry

Chickpeas and spinach in a warmly spiced coconut tomato sauce, spooned over fluffy basmati rice.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Total
30 minutes
Serves
3

Ingredients

  • 180 g basmati rice
  • Water for rice, as directed on its packet
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 1 can (425 g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (400 g) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (400 ml) full-fat coconut milk
  • 90 g baby spinach
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Rinse the rice. Cook with the water quantity and timing on its packet. Remove from the heat and keep covered while you finish the curry.

  2. 02

    Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Cook the onion for 5 minutes until softened. Stir in the curry powder and cook for 30 seconds.

  3. 03

    Add the tomatoes, chickpeas, coconut milk, salt and pepper. Bring to a gentle simmer, then cook uncovered for 12–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce coats a spoon.

  4. 04

    Fold in the spinach and cook for 2 minutes until wilted. Taste the sauce and adjust the salt. Fluff the rice and serve the curry alongside it.
