30-Minute Chickpea and Coconut Milk Curry with Basmati Rice

Creamy coconut curry, tender chickpeas, tomatoes, and spinach over fluffy basmati rice—comforting enough for dinner and practical enough for a weeknight.

Recipe by Nibble Published September 3, 2026

Ingredients Method
Prep
8 minutes
Cook
22 minutes
Total
30 minutes
Serves
4

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice, rinsed
  • 1½ cups water
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tablespoon mild curry powder
  • 1 can (15 ounces) chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 1 can (14 ounces) diced tomatoes, with their juices
  • 1 can (13.5 ounces) coconut milk
  • 3 cups fresh baby spinach
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt, divided
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

How to make it

Method

  1. 01

    Cook the rice

    Add the rinsed rice, water, and ¼ teaspoon salt to a saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook undisturbed for 15 minutes. Remove from the heat, let stand covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.

  2. 02

    Soften the onion

    While the rice cooks, heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened.

  3. 03

    Bloom the spices

    Add the garlic, ginger, and curry powder. Cook for 30 to 60 seconds, stirring, until fragrant.

  4. 04

    Simmer the curry

    Stir in the chickpeas, diced tomatoes with their juices, coconut milk, remaining ¾ teaspoon salt, and black pepper. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 10 to 12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.

  5. 05

    Wilt the spinach and serve

    Fold in the spinach and cook for 1 to 2 minutes, until wilted. Taste and adjust the seasoning, then spoon the curry over the fluffy basmati rice and serve hot.

One useful detail

Keep the curry at a gentle simmer after adding the coconut milk. A hard boil can make the sauce look split instead of creamy.
