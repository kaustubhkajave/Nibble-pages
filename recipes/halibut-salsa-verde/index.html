Halibut & Salsa Verde

Golden pan-seared halibut on crispy potato rounds, striped with a bright herb and caper salsa verde and finished with lemon zest.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 halibut fillets (5–6 oz / 150–170 g each)
  • 2 medium Yukon Gold potatoes, sliced 1/2 in (1 cm) thick
  • 1 cup (30 g) parsley leaves
  • 1 tbsp capers, drained
  • 1 anchovy fillet (optional)
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 1 lemon
  • 6 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Boil the potato slices in salted water for 6–8 minutes until just tender. Drain and pat dry.

  2. 02

    Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and fry the potato rounds for 3–4 minutes per side until crisp. Season with a pinch of salt and keep warm.

  3. 03

    Very finely chop the parsley, capers, anchovy and garlic. Stir in 3 tablespoons oil, the juice of half the lemon and a pinch of salt.

  4. 04

    Pat the halibut dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat the remaining oil over medium-high heat and sear for 3–4 minutes until golden, then turn and cook for 2–3 minutes until it flakes easily and reaches 145°F (63°C).

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Crispy potato rounds

    Overlap 3 crispy potato rounds in a 4-inch (10 cm) diagonal arc across the center.

  2. Step 2

    Pan-seared halibut

    Set the halibut diagonally on top of the potatoes, golden side up.

  3. Step 3

    Salsa verde

    Spoon 2 tablespoons of salsa verde in a neat ribbon down the center of the fish.

  4. Step 4

    Salsa verde

    Spoon 1 tablespoon of salsa verde into 3 small dots along the bottom edge of the potatoes.

  5. Step 5

    Lemon zest

    Finish with 5 fine strands of lemon zest over the fish.
