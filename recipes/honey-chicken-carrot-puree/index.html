Honey Chicken & Carrot Purée

Crisp-skinned chicken thighs with a honey-garlic glaze, over a velvety carrot-ginger purée with charred broccolini.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skin-on chicken thighs, about 1 1/4 lb (550 g) total
  • 3 medium carrots, about 10 oz (300 g), sliced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 bunch broccolini, about 7 oz (200 g)
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided, plus salt for the cooking water
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the carrots and ginger in salted water for 12–15 minutes until very soft. Drain, keeping a little of the water, and blend with 1 tablespoon butter and 2–3 tablespoons of the cooking water until glossy. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Pat the chicken dry and season with 1/2 teaspoon salt and the pepper. Lay it skin-side down in a cold skillet with the oil and set over medium heat. Cook for 10–12 minutes, without moving, until the skin is crisp and golden.

  3. 03

    Turn the chicken and cook for 5–6 minutes more, until it reaches 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part. Transfer to a plate.

  4. 04

    Add the broccolini to the same pan and cook for 4–5 minutes, turning once, until charred in places and just tender. Season with a pinch of salt and set aside.

  5. 05

    Lower the heat. Add the garlic, honey, the remaining butter and the juice of half the lemon to the pan and bubble for about 1 minute into a glossy glaze. Brush it over the chicken skin, then slice each thigh. Keep the remaining glaze for plating.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Carrot-ginger purée

    Spoon a 4-inch (10 cm) round pool of carrot purée slightly left of center on each plate.

  2. Step 2

    Charred broccolini

    Lay 3 broccolini spears across the right side of the purée, stems pointing toward the lower right.

  3. Step 3

    Honey-glazed chicken

    Arrange the slices of one chicken thigh, overlapping, diagonally over the broccolini.

  4. Step 4

    Pan glaze

    Spoon about 1 tablespoon of the pan glaze over the chicken, letting a little run onto the purée.

  5. Step 5

    Lemon zest

    Finish with a little lemon zest over the chicken and wipe the rim clean.
