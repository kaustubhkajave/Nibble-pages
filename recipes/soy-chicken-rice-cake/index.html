Soy Chicken & Rice Cake

Soy-honey glazed chicken thighs over a crisp jasmine rice cake, with cool cucumber ribbons, curled scallion threads and toasted sesame.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 300 g cold cooked jasmine rice, about 2 cups
  • 4 boneless skinless chicken thighs, about 450 g, each cut into 3 pieces
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 Persian cucumber
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • 3 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Season the rice with the salt. Press it very firmly into two 10 cm rings or tight discs about 2 cm thick. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Fry the discs for 5–6 minutes per side without moving them until golden and crisp. Turn carefully with a wide spatula; hot oil can spatter. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Shave the cucumber lengthwise into ribbons with a peeler. Slice the scallions very thinly on a long angle into threads and soak them in ice water for 5 minutes to curl, then drain well. Toast the sesame seeds in a dry pan for 1–2 minutes until golden.

  3. 03

    Pat the chicken dry and season with the pepper. Heat the remaining oil in the skillet over medium-high heat. Sear for 4–5 minutes per side until well browned and the thickest piece reaches 74°C / 165°F.

  4. 04

    Mix the soy sauce and honey. Turn the heat to medium, pour it over the cooked chicken and simmer for 1–2 minutes, turning the pieces, until the glaze thickens and coats them. Take the pan off the heat before the glaze darkens.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Crispy rice cake

    Set one crisp rice cake in the center of each plate.

  2. Step 2

    Glazed chicken

    Pile the glazed chicken neatly over the rice cake, spooning a little glaze from the pan over the top.

  3. Step 3

    Cucumber ribbons

    Fold three cucumber ribbons into a tight curve along the lower-right edge of the rice cake.

  4. Step 4

    Scallion threads

    Pile a small mound of scallion threads on the center of the chicken.

  5. Step 5

    Toasted sesame

    Sprinkle the toasted sesame seeds over the chicken and the edge of the rice cake. Follow the thermometer guidance in the cooking steps, regardless of the color shown in the image.
