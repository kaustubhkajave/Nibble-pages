Crispy Eggs & Chickpeas

Lacy-edged fried eggs and crunchy chickpeas over cool garlic yogurt, with a warm chili butter drizzle and fresh dill.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
20 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 250 g Greek yogurt, at room temperature
  • 1 can chickpeas, about 400 g, drained and patted very dry
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 30 g unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • 3 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 8 small dill fronds

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Stir the yogurt with half the garlic and 1/4 teaspoon salt until smooth. Leave at room temperature.

  2. 02

    Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chickpeas in a single layer and fry for 6–8 minutes, shaking the pan occasionally, until golden and crisp. Stand back; they can pop and spatter. Drain on kitchen paper and season with a pinch of salt.

  3. 03

    Melt the butter in a small pan over low heat with the remaining garlic for about 1 minute. Take it off the heat and stir in the chili flakes. Keep warm.

  4. 04

    Heat the remaining oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Crack in the eggs and cook for 2–3 minutes, spooning the hot oil over the whites, until the edges are lacy and the whites are fully set. For firm yolks, cover the pan for 1–2 minutes more.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Garlic yogurt

    Spoon half the garlic yogurt into the center of each plate and smooth it into a bold 12 cm circle.

  2. Step 2

    Crispy fried eggs

    Set two fried eggs side by side on the upper center of the yogurt, their crispy edges slightly overlapping.

  3. Step 3

    Crispy chickpeas

    Spoon the crispy chickpeas around the right and lower edges of the eggs.

  4. Step 4

    Chili butter

    Drizzle the warm chili butter over the egg whites and around the inner edge of the yogurt.

  5. Step 5

    Dill

    Tuck four small dill fronds onto the eggs and chickpeas.
