Gochujang Steak & Spinach

Gochujang-glazed flank steak over a garlic and sesame spinach purée, with crisp pickled radish.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340 g) flank steak
  • 5 cups (150 g) fresh spinach
  • 6 radishes, thinly sliced or shaved
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 4 tbsp water, divided

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Toss the radishes with the rice vinegar, the sugar and a pinch of salt. Set aside for 15 minutes.

  2. 02

    Blanch the spinach in boiling water for 20 seconds, cool under cold water and squeeze dry. Blend with 1 garlic clove, the sesame oil, a pinch of salt and 2 tablespoons water until smooth.

  3. 03

    Simmer the gochujang, soy sauce, honey, 1 minced garlic clove and 2 tablespoons water for 1–2 minutes until glossy.

  4. 04

    Pat the steak dry and season lightly with salt. Heat the oil in a skillet over high heat and sear for 3–4 minutes per side, until the thickest part reaches 145°F (63°C). Rest for at least 3 minutes.

  5. 05

    Slice the steak against the grain and brush the slices with the glaze.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Garlic spinach purée

    Spoon about 4 tablespoons of spinach purée onto the left of each plate and sweep it into a broad curve.

  2. Step 2

    Seared flank steak

    Fan 5 slices of steak diagonally across the top of the purée.

  3. Step 3

    Pickled radish

    Nest 3 curls of pickled radish along the upper right edge of the steak.

  4. Step 4

    Gochujang glaze

    Spoon 1 tablespoon of gochujang glaze over the steak only.

  5. Step 5

    Pickled radish

    Top the middle slice with 2 small radish curls.
