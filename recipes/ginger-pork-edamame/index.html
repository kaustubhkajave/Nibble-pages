Ginger Pork & Edamame

Golden pork tenderloin medallions over bright edamame purée, with snap peas, red pepper, carrot ribbons and ginger-soy glaze.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
35 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340 g) pork tenderloin, cut into 6 medallions
  • 1 1/2 cups (225 g) frozen shelled edamame
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into thin strips
  • 2 oz (60 g) sugar snap peas, halved lengthwise
  • 1 carrot, shaved into ribbons
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) water, plus water for boiling
  • 1/2 tsp salt, plus salt for boiling

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Boil the edamame in salted water for 4–5 minutes. Drain and blend with 3 tbsp water, 1 tbsp oil and a pinch of salt until smooth and bright green.

  2. 02

    Season the pork with the remaining salt. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and sear the medallions for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and they reach 145°F (63°C). Rest for 3 minutes.

  3. 03

    Lower the heat, add the ginger, soy sauce, sugar and 1 tbsp water to the pan and simmer for 1 minute, scraping up the browned bits, until glossy.

  4. 04

    In another pan, stir-fry the pepper, snap peas and carrot ribbons in the remaining oil for 90 seconds until bright.

  5. 05

    Spread the edamame purée in an oval on each plate. Overlap three pork medallions along the top, add the vegetables at the lower right, drape a few carrot ribbons over the pork and spoon the ginger glaze over it.
