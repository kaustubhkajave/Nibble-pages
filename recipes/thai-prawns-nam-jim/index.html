Thai Prawns & Nam Jim

Golden seared prawns over a punchy cilantro-chili-lime sauce, with blistered peppers, shallots and cool cucumber ribbons.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps
Get the Nibble app
Total
25 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340 g) jumbo prawns or shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 large bunch cilantro
  • 1–2 green chilies, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 limes
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into strips
  • 2 shallots, sliced
  • 1 small cucumber, shaved into ribbons
  • 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil, divided
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Blend the cilantro, green chilies, garlic, juice of both limes, sugar, water and half the salt until smooth.

  2. 02

    Heat 1 tbsp oil in a skillet over high heat. Blister the pepper and shallots for 3–4 minutes and set aside.

  3. 03

    Season the prawns with the remaining salt and sear in the remaining oil for 1 1/2–2 minutes per side until golden, pink and opaque all the way through.

  4. 04

    Spoon a pool of nam jim on each plate, overlap the prawns along its upper edge, pile the peppers and shallots beside them, curl the cucumber ribbons around the lower edge and drizzle a little more sauce over the prawns.
