Crispy Salmon & Pea Purée

Crispy-skinned salmon on a mint and pea purée, with lemony shaved radish and a bright lemon-mint oil.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
25 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 skin-on salmon fillets, 5–6 oz (150–170 g) each
  • 1 1/2 cups (200 g) frozen peas
  • Small bunch of mint, 6 tiny leaves reserved
  • 4 radishes
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Simmer the peas with the garlic in a little water for 3 minutes. Drain, keeping some of the water, and blend with the butter, 4 mint leaves, 1 teaspoon lemon juice, a pinch of salt and 2–3 tablespoons of the water until silky. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Shave the radishes thinly with a peeler or mandoline and toss with 1 teaspoon lemon juice and a pinch of salt.

  3. 03

    Chop 4 mint leaves very finely and stir into 2 tablespoons oil with the zest of the lemon.

  4. 04

    Pat the salmon very dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook skin-side down for 5–6 minutes, pressing gently for the first 30 seconds, until the skin is crisp.

  5. 05

    Turn and cook for 1–2 minutes, until the thickest part reaches 145°F (63°C) and flakes easily.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Mint pea purée

    Spoon about 4 tablespoons of pea purée across the center of each plate and drag it into a 5-inch (13 cm) diagonal swoosh.

  2. Step 2

    Crispy salmon

    Set one salmon fillet, skin-side up, on the upper part of the swoosh at an angle.

  3. Step 3

    Lemon radish ribbons

    Nest 4 radish ribbons against the left side of the salmon.

  4. Step 4

    Mint leaves

    Tuck 3 tiny mint leaves into the folds of the radish.

  5. Step 5

    Lemon-mint oil

    Spoon 1 teaspoon of lemon-mint oil over the top of the salmon.
