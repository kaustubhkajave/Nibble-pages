Chicken Thighs & Red Pepper

Crisp golden chicken thighs on a glossy red pepper purée, with seared zucchini ribbons, basil oil and lemon zest.

Recipe by Nibble

Ingredients Cooking steps Plating photos
Total
40 minutes
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skin-on chicken thighs, about 1 1/4 lb (550 g) total
  • 2 red bell peppers (or 1 cup / 150 g jarred roasted peppers)
  • 1 small zucchini
  • Large handful of basil (about 1 cup / 20 g), 10 small leaves reserved
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 3/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Cooking steps

  1. 01

    Char the bell peppers under a hot broiler, turning, until blistered all over, or use jarred peppers. Peel, seed and blend with the garlic, the butter, 1 teaspoon lemon juice and 1/4 teaspoon salt until smooth. Keep warm.

  2. 02

    Blend the basil with 3 tablespoons oil and a pinch of salt, then strain through a fine sieve for a bright basil oil.

  3. 03

    Pat the chicken dry and season with the remaining salt and the pepper. Lay it skin-side down in a cold skillet with the remaining oil and set over medium heat. Cook for 12–14 minutes, without moving, until the skin is deep golden.

  4. 04

    Turn the chicken and cook for 5–6 minutes more, until it reaches 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part. Transfer to a plate.

  5. 05

    Slice the zucchini lengthwise into thin ribbons and sear them in the chicken fat for 1–2 minutes per side.

Plate it up

  1. Step 1

    Red pepper purée

    Spoon a generous 5-inch (13 cm) pool of red pepper purée slightly left of center on each plate.

  2. Step 2

    Crispy chicken thighs

    Overlap 2 chicken thighs in the center of the purée, skin-side up.

  3. Step 3

    Seared zucchini ribbons

    Fold 3 seared zucchini ribbons and tuck them around the right side of the chicken.

  4. Step 4

    Basil oil

    Spoon 1 tablespoon of basil oil over the chicken, letting it run onto the purée.

  5. Step 5

    Basil and lemon zest

    Top with 5 small basil leaves and a fine sprinkle of lemon zest.
