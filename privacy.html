The short version

Nibble has no user accounts. Saved recipes and preferences stay on your device. When you use an AI feature, the content needed for that request is sent to Nibble's API and the relevant service provider. We do not sell personal information or use advertising trackers.

What stays on your device

Saved recipes, their optional public source links, folders, recent cooking history, preferences, onboarding status, and an anonymous installation identifier are stored locally. Nibble does not provide in-app cloud syncing. Deleting the app removes this data from that device; any device backups are controlled by Apple and your iOS settings.

A selected ingredient photo remains temporary app data and is not restored after an app restart.

What is processed when you use Nibble

Information Purpose Handling
Ingredient photo Identify food and pantry items in the selected image. Sent to Nibble's API and Google Gemini. Nibble does not save the image to its database.
Confirmed ingredients, preferences, and dish names Create the recipes you request. Sent to Nibble's API and an AI provider, currently OpenAI. Nibble does not save this content to its database.
Recipe text and recipe website links Import a recipe from written ingredients and directions. Processed transiently by Nibble's API and the relevant provider. Original recipe text and webpage contents are not saved. If you save the recipe, its structured content and optional public source link stay on your device.
Anonymous installation identifier Apply free usage, verify subscriptions, limit abuse, and locate requests when you provide a Support ID. Sent to Nibble's API and RevenueCat. Nibble stores a keyed hash rather than the original identifier in server records.
Connection and request metadata Secure, operate, and troubleshoot the service. Includes items such as app version, platform, endpoint, request ID, status, timing, model usage, and a hashed IP address. Recipe content is excluded from Nibble's logs.
Subscription information Confirm whether Nibble Plus is active. Apple handles payment details. RevenueCat provides product, entitlement, and renewal status to Nibble.
Support communications Respond when you contact us. Your email address, message, and any Support ID you choose to include are retained in our support mailbox as needed to resolve the request.

AI processing and retention

Nibble's API keeps request content in memory only long enough to complete the request. It does not place ingredient photos, ingredient names, dietary text, dish names, recipe text, recipe webpage contents, or generated recipes in its operational logs or database.

AI providers may temporarily retain inputs and outputs for security, abuse monitoring, and legal compliance under their own terms. OpenAI states that API content may be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring unless different controls apply. Google's paid Gemini services may log prompts and responses for a limited period for abuse monitoring. Nibble does not opt in to use request content to train provider models.

Service providers

  • Google Gemini processes ingredient photos.
  • OpenAI processes confirmed ingredients, preferences, dish names, and pasted recipe text for recipe generation.
  • Recipe websites receive a standard request from Nibble's server when you import one of their public pages. They may process that request under their own privacy policies.
  • Google Cloud hosts Nibble's API and server-side operational records.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription entitlement status using the anonymous installation identifier.
  • Apple processes App Store payments and purchase history. Nibble does not receive your payment card details.

These providers process information only to supply, secure, or support the requested service and operate under their own privacy and data-protection terms. They may process information in countries other than your own.

Retention and deletion

Operational logs and hashed server records are retained only while reasonably needed to operate the service, enforce usage and subscriptions, prevent abuse, resolve support issues, and meet legal obligations. Support emails are retained as needed to handle the conversation and related records.

Delete the app to erase Nibble's local data from your device. You can also remove camera or photo-library access in iOS Settings and continue by entering ingredients manually. To request deletion of server-side records or support communications, email getnibble@outlook.com and include your Support ID when available.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant records without creating an account for you.

Website privacy

These Nibble information pages do not use analytics, advertising pixels, cookies, or contact forms. If you email us, your email provider and Outlook process that communication.

Children

Nibble is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Nibble's features, providers, or legal obligations change. The date at the top will identify the latest version.
