Privacy Policy

Last updated [[EFFECTIVE DATE]]. This policy covers the Nibble iOS app and the API it talks to.

The short version. Nibble has no accounts. Your saved recipes, folders, and preferences live on your device, not on a server. Photos and ingredient lists are sent to be processed into recipes and are not kept afterwards. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

What stays on your device

Saved recipes, recipe folders, favourites, recently cooked meals, your dinner preferences, and onboarding state are stored in local app storage. They are not uploaded, backed up to us, or readable by us. If you delete the app, they are gone.

What is sent when you generate a recipe

What When Why
An ingredient photo When you take or choose one To identify ingredients. Processed and discarded — the image is not stored by us.
Confirmed ingredient names and your preferences When you ask for meals To generate recipes that match your time, effort, and dietary constraints.
A dish name When you use Create Recipe To generate that specific recipe.
An anonymous install identifier Every request A random ID created on your device. It is hashed before use so requests can be rate-limited fairly. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID, or device identifiers.

What our logs contain

Our server keeps operational logs — which endpoint was called, whether it succeeded, how long it took, and roughly what it cost. These logs are deliberately built not to contain your content: dish names, ingredient names, and dietary text are excluded, and we have automated tests that fail the build if they ever appear.

Who else processes it

  • Google (Gemini) — ingredient recognition from your photo.
  • OpenAI — recipe generation from your confirmed ingredients and preferences.
  • Google Cloud — hosting for our API.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status, if you subscribe. It receives the same anonymous install identifier.
  • Apple — handles all payments. We never see your payment details.

These providers process data on our behalf to deliver the feature you asked for. We do not send them anything that identifies you personally, because we do not have anything that identifies you personally.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, passwords, names, or email addresses.
  • No advertising, ad networks, or tracking across other apps.
  • No selling or sharing of data with data brokers.
  • No location, contacts, or health data.

Photos permission

Nibble asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to add a photo. It reads the single image you select. It does not browse or index your library.

Children

Nibble is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and rights

Because there is no account, the fastest way to erase everything associated with your device is to delete the app — that removes all locally stored recipes and preferences along with the anonymous install identifier. If you would like us to delete server-side rate limiting or subscription records, or if you have a question about your data under GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and the updated version will be posted here.
