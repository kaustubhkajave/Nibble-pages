How it works

1. Show what you have

Take a photo, pick one from your library, or type ingredients in by hand. You confirm the list before anything is generated.

2. Set the rules

Time you have, effort, cleanup, dietary needs, and how many meals you want. Nibble only suggests things that fit.

3. Cook it

Step-by-step cooking mode with timers, an overview whenever you want the whole picture, and a library to save what worked.

Built to be honest

Nibble tells you when it can't do something. If your ingredients only stretch to one good meal, you get one good meal and a clear explanation — not three padded ones.

Recipes and preferences are stored on your device. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to. See the privacy policy for exactly what is sent where.

Nibble Plus

Everyone gets a few recipes free. Nibble Plus adds a larger monthly or weekly allowance of AI-generated recipes. Prices are shown in your local currency in the app before you subscribe, and you can cancel any time in App Store settings.
