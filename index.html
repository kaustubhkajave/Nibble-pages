What does Nibble do?

Nibble turns the ingredients you already have into recipes. Photograph your fridge, pantry, or counter—or type the list—confirm what Nibble found, then choose servings, time, effort, and cleanup.

Do I need an account?

No. Open Nibble and start cooking. Saved recipes and preferences stay on your device. The privacy policy explains what the service processes.

Is Nibble free?

A new installation includes three free recipe credits. Each generated meal uses one; photo analysis, saved recipes, and cooking do not. Nibble Plus adds unlimited recipe generation.

What is Chef Mode?

Chef Mode creates a more elevated version of a dish from the same ingredients, with dedicated cooking and plating steps. Choose Chef It Up before generating, or elevate an everyday recipe later.

What if the photo misses something?

Add, rename, or remove ingredients on the review screen before generating. Only the list you confirm is used.

Nibble Plus

Unlimited recipes. Chef images included.

Weekly Plus includes 5 Chef images per subscription period; Monthly Plus includes 20. Need more? One-time image packs add non-expiring credits. Prices and renewal terms appear before purchase.
