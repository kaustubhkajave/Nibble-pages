How Nibble works

Cook what you have. Start with a photo of your fridge.

Open the fridge, take one photo, and Nibble lists the ingredients it can see. Check the list, tell it how much time and effort you have, and pick from up to three dinners built around what’s already there.

Download on the App Store ↗ See the six steps ↓
  • No account
  • You approve every ingredient
  • iPhone app
  1. 01

    Snap the fridge

    Take a photo of the fridge, the pantry shelf or whatever is on the counter. You can also pick a photo you already have, or type the ingredients in yourself if you’d rather not use the camera.

    • Take a photo or choose one from Photos
    • Or enter ingredients manually
  2. 02

    Check what Nibble found

    Nibble lists every ingredient it spotted, with rough amounts where it can tell—“eggs · 3 cartons”, “carrots · 1 bowl”. Rename anything it got wrong, remove what you don’t want used, and add what the photo missed.

    Only the list you confirm goes into your recipes.

  3. 03

    Set tonight’s preferences

    Tell Nibble what kind of night it is. Choose a recipe style, then fit the meal to your evening.

    Recipe style
    Everyday for practical cooking, or Chef It Up for elevated technique and plating
    How many meals
    1, 2 or 3 ideas to choose from
    Servings
    1, 2, 4 or 6
    Time you have
    10, 20, 30 or 45+ minutes
    Effort
    Very easy or Normal
    Cleanup
    One pan, Minimal or Any

    Switch on No grocery trip to prefer meals with nothing essential missing, and add any dietary needs.

  4. 04

    Pick from three dinners that need nothing extra

    From one fridge of eggs, vegetables and condiments, Nibble came back with an herb omelet, tomato-pepper eggs and crisp vegetable egg cakes—three genuinely different dinners. Each card shows the time, effort and cleanup, what it uses from your kitchen, anything missing, and why the dish works.

    • “Nothing to buy” when every essential is already in your kitchen
    • If your list can’t stretch that far, Nibble says what small extras each recipe needs
    • Fewer ingredients may mean fewer ideas—Nibble won’t pad the list with filler
  5. 05

    Cook one step at a time

    Tap Start Cooking and Nibble shows one step at a time, in big type you can read from the stove. Each step lists the ingredients you need right now, and steps that need timing come with a timer.

    • Progress bar so you always know how far along you are
    • “Needed now” ingredients on every step
    • Built-in timers where they matter
  6. 06

    Plate it, step by step

    Chef It Up dinners don’t stop when the cooking does. Once dinner’s cooked, Nibble switches to a separate plating phase: short actions, one component at a time, with a final-plate reference to aim for.

    Plate photos are AI-generated inspiration. Your finished dish may differ.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

More help in Support →
What if the photo misses something?

Add, rename, or remove ingredients on the review screen before generating. Only the list you confirm is used.

Do I have to use a photo?

No. You can choose an existing photo, or skip the camera and enter your ingredients manually.

Will I always get three dinners?

You choose one, two or three. If your ingredients only stretch to fewer good meals, Nibble shows fewer rather than inventing filler, and suggests adding a few more ingredients.

Does Nibble know about my allergies?

You can add dietary needs on the preferences screen, but Nibble can make mistakes. Always check ingredients, labels, allergies and dietary requirements yourself.

Is Nibble free?

A new installation includes three free recipe credits. Each generated meal uses one; photo analysis, saved recipes, and cooking do not. Nibble Plus adds unlimited recipe generation.
